### `--likert-only` removal (A1)

**AUDIT VERDICT (confirmed by inspection this session):** `--likert-only` can be removed **COMPLETELY**, with **NO backward-compat shim** and **NO fixture change** — it is a **pure deletion + doc-scrub**. It lands in **Phase 119** alongside the seed work (per the A1 ripple note: 119 fixtures / 119.3 spec-edit allowance / 130.4 seed). This is the **highest-leverage cross-cutting finding** because it ripples into Phase 119 fixtures, 119.3, and the Phase 130 seed.
