## baseV1 Dataset Surprises Observed

**(One paragraph per the PLAN's empirical-flake gate.)**

The walkthrough surfaced two baseV1 dataset contract refinements vs the original Plan 88-01 inventory that future 88-NN broader refactor must absorb. **(1) Hidden-candidate exclusion is not enforced** — CA-AA-Hidden has `terms_of_use_accepted` deliberately absent per baseV1.ts:839, yet it appears in the candidate-list ranked at 100% match. The `voter-matching.spec.ts:280-285` equivalent assertion against the e2e dataset passes (e.g. confirms exclusion is implemented and contract-true) which means the gap is settings-driven: `BASE_V1_APP_SETTINGS` (baseV1.ts:142-233) lacks whatever toggle the e2e settings carry to filter out un-accepted-terms candidates. **(2) CA-AA-Special is not the perfect-match candidate** — refactor-doc:320-328 (and the Plan inventory step 12 row) named CA-AA-Special as the perfect-match top result. Empirically, the POLAR_MAX candidates (`test-ca-aa-1` "Generic AA One" at baseV1.ts:851-861, plus the un-filtered CA-AA-Hidden at :836-849) rank at 100% match while CA-AA-Special ranks at 80% because its asymmetric answer arrangement (case b/c/d per the voter-vs-entity matrix at baseV1.ts:817-832) reduces its score. The matrix-arrangement design (CA-AA-Special as the *partial-answer* candidate exercising the 4 cases on the detail drawer) is in direct tension with the *perfect-match* role the original spec inventory assigned it. Additionally, the multi-election dispatch surfaced an unanticipated UI step: `/results` lands on an AccordionSelect election-picker ("Select an election first") when `dataRoot.elections.length > 1`, and the user must pick an election before the candidate / party section renders — `voter-mega.fixture.ts` does not currently handle this, so any future migration of mega-journey-style tests will need to absorb the picker dispatch. Two minor surprises: the "Filtered Mun-NE" question name was not observed in the body text during the walk despite the constituency-scoping contract (refactor-doc:280, may be a category-intro-display-vs-question-render distinction); and case (d) "Neither has answered" message did not surface on CA-AA-Special's opinionsTab (Filt-Mun-NE may not reach this candidate's section).
