## Problem

DaisyUI 5.7.46 declares, in `node_modules/daisyui/components/label.css`:

```css
.label{white-space:nowrap;color:color-mix(in oklab, currentcolor 60%, transparent);align-items:center;gap:.375rem;display:inline-flex}
```

`white-space: nowrap` makes a label's `min-content` width its whole line. Where the label is a flex item of a centred column, the line cannot shrink and the page grows around it, half of the overflow to the left, where a document cannot scroll. That is what put the terms-of-use checkbox off-screen on a phone; Phase 174.1 measured it (`174.1-RESEARCH.md` § M1, M2) and fixed that one label with `whitespace-normal`.

Every other element carrying the `label` class and holding free text is one long string away from the same thing. The users today, derived on 2026-10-07 at `1c5d2896d` by searching `apps/frontend/src` for class attributes that hold the token `label` (the `small-label` utility left out):

| User | Elements | Phone width |
|------|----------|-------------|
| `lib/contestant/components/termsOfUse/TermsOfUseForm.svelte` | 1 | fixed by Phase 174.1; wraps in all seven locales at 320, 390, 640, 740 and 1280 px (`174.1-EVIDENCE.md` § Reads) |
| `lib/components/electionSelector/ElectionSelector.svelte` | 1 | measured by the research: fits at 320 and 390 with the dataset's names (widest 226.2 px); computed `nowrap`, so an election or constituency name longer than the column overflows |

What is and is not known:

- None of the measured users overflows with the `e2e/base` dataset. The strings are data (election names, filter options) or translations, so a deployment, or a locale, can make one longer than a phone is wide. In the terms-of-use view, before the fix, the same sentence fitted at 640 px in three locales and overflowed in four (`174.1-RESEARCH.md` § M3).
- The measurements above are the research's, on 2026-10-07 on the tree before the fix; plan 174.1-02 re-measured the terms-of-use label only.
- The voter questions page's label and the Admin App's three files were never opened at a phone width.
- `apps/frontend/src/app.css` already overrides one declaration of the same rule: `.label { color: inherit; }` in the `utilities` layer, outside DaisyUI's nested sublayer, restoring the contrast the rule's 60% mix takes away. A plain utility on the element also beats `.label`: `whitespace-normal` computes `normal` on the terms-of-use label (`174.1-EVIDENCE.md`, relation G1).

## Solution

Two ways out, both weighed by the research (`174.1-RESEARCH.md` § *Recommended fixes*, the rejected alternatives):

1. **`whitespace-normal` on each label that holds free text.** One class per element, as Phase 174.1 did. Cost: every such label has to be found and a new one can forget it; where a label wraps beside a checkbox the box is centred on the lines unless the label also takes `!items-start`, as `EnumeratedEntityFilter.svelte` already does.
2. **One global rule beside the colour override in `app.css`:** `.label { white-space: normal; }`. Covers every user, present and future. Cost: it changes every label in the voter, contestant and admin apps at once, including any that rely on staying on one line, so it needs a look at each user above at a phone width and at 1280 px, and the visual baselines regenerated if a snapshot holds one.

Whichever is taken:

- Open the unchecked users at 320 and 390 px first: the voter questions page and the three Admin App files.
- Hold the result with a geometric check (`scrollWidth` against `documentElement.clientWidth`), not an axe scan, which reports no violation on this overflow (`174.1-RESEARCH.md` § M10).
- If the global rule is taken, the class on the terms-of-use label becomes redundant and can go.

## Update 2026-10-09 — the label of an `Input` row (Phase 175, `175-12-OVERFLOW-FIX.md`)

One more user was found, and it was not latent: `inputLabelClass` in `apps/frontend/src/lib/components/input/shared.ts`, the label inside every single-row `Input`. It was fixed by way 1 above, on the constant: `whitespace-normal`, by the orchestrator's ruling (operator decision B5, pending).

**What was measured before the fix** (runs `175-12-overflow-probe-base` and `175-12-overflow-probe`):

- An organization's profile was 84 px wider than the viewport at 320 px and 15 px at 390 px, in `en`, `fi` and `sv`, for the confirmed and the unconfirmed organization alike. The row of the locked info question `[QU-INFO-ORG-LOCKED] Registration number` is inside an `InputGroup`, whose `<fieldset>` computes `min-inline-size: min-content`; with the label's computed `white-space: nowrap` the group was 383.8 px wide whatever the viewport. `white-space: normal` on that one label, as a throwaway inline style, took the overflow to 0 at both widths.
- Outside a group the same label does not widen the page: the label carries `min-w-[4rem]`, so the row squeezes its box and the one-line text runs out of the box, across the control, until the row clips it. On the base candidate's profile six info-question labels did that at 320 px (one-line text of 230 to 542 px in boxes of 158 to 204 px) and at 390 px, and one did at 1280 px (542 px of text in a 446 px box). Read on the fixed tree with the one line forced back by an inline style, which reproduced the unfixed tree's boxes exactly.

**What the fix does** (run `175-12-overflow-probe-after-r2`): the organization's profile has no overflow at 320 and 390 px in the three locales; the labels above wrap inside the box the row gives them; no label, control or row moved sideways on any measured page at any width. At 1280 px the one change is that one label of the base candidate's profile takes two lines.

**What remains**

- Every other user in the table above is untouched and as it was: `ElectionSelector`, the two entity filters, the voter questions page and the three Admin App files.
- **One word wider than the room.** A label that is a single word the browser cannot hyphenate still cannot wrap. Inside an `InputGroup` it widens the page again: a 39-letter word, 296 px wide, as the label of the locked info question's row gave 50 px of overflow at 320 px on an `en` page and none at 390 px. On a `fi` and an `sv` page the same word was hyphenated by the browser (`html` carries `hyphens: auto`) and gave none. `overflow-wrap: anywhere` closes it and was measured and rejected: a row gives a label a few pixels less than its text before the control is at its narrowest, and with it "Constituency" at 320 px broke into "Constituenc" and "y".
- **A one-word label a few pixels narrower than its text** runs those pixels into its own margin, as before the fix: "Constituency" is 93 px of text in an 89 px box at 320 px. Not a defect anyone would see; it is why the previous point has no cheap fix.
- **A different overflow, found beside this one:** a candidate's profile that holds a multiple-text info question is 8 px wider than the viewport at 320 px. The cause is the row list's three reorder and remove buttons, not a label; it is item 4 of `.planning/phases/175-organization-editing-profile-questions-preview/deferred-items.md`.
- If the global rule of way 2 is taken, `whitespace-normal` on `inputLabelClass` becomes redundant, as the class on the terms-of-use label does.

## Update 2026-10-11 — the election selector's label wraps, measured; the other users stand (Phase 177 close)

`ElectionSelector`'s option label now carries `whitespace-normal` (plan 177-20, commit `2f32e988c`). Measured before the fix with an 89-character election name: the election step of the nomination editor was 177 px too wide at 320 px and 142 px at 390 px, and, because the selector is a centred item of a column, the labels began at x = −177 and x = −142, so **every checkbox was beyond the viewport's left edge and could not be checked** (`tests/e2e-runs/177-e2e/177-20-control`). That is the consequence this todo names for the terms-of-use label, measured for a second user. After the fix the page fits at both widths, the label shows its whole text inside the viewport and the checkbox can be checked (`phone 320:` and `phone 390: the election step fits the viewport with a long election name`, green in the closing run). So this todo's table has one user fewer: the row that says the selector fits only with the dataset's short names is measured and fixed. The component is shared with the voter's election page and the pre-registration page; **neither was opened with a long name**, the fix there is inferred from the shared class. The other label users of the table are untouched and stand as described.
