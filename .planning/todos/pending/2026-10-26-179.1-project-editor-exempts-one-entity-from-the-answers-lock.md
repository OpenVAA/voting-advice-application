## Problem

The answers lock is project-wide. While a project's `access.answersLocked` setting is on, the database refuses every entity-scope write of an entity's own editor: its answers, its profile fields, its nominations and its files. The Contestant App shows every one of those editors the not-editable note.

A project editor may want one entity to keep editing after the lock comes on. Examples: a candidate whose late correction the organisers have agreed to, or a party admitted after the deadline. Today the only ways are to edit on the entity's behalf with a project grant, or to turn the lock off for everyone.

## What exists

- `private.project_answers_locked(p_project_id)` reads `'{access,answersLocked}'` from the project's `app_settings`. A missing project reads as locked.
- It is a conjunct of the entity-scope policies only: the four `entity_update_own_*` policies and the four `entity_*_nominations` policies. `storage_path_can` repeats the check inline in its entity arm. `upsert_answers` and `set_entity_nominations` raise `42501` with `answers are locked in this project` for a caller without the project grant.
- Project grant holders (`project.edit_entities`, `project.edit_nominations`) write through the project-scope policies, which never read the lock, so they pass.
- The frontend reads the lock from the settings alone (`contestantCtx.answersLocked`). The data writer maps the refusal to `AnswersLockedError` and to the `answersLocked` refusal of `setEntityNominations`.

## Direction

- A per-entity exemption that a project editor sets, for example a column on the entity tables or a small table keyed by entity, written only through a project-scope policy.
- The same conjunct reads it: the helper takes the entity as well as the project, or a second helper is AND-ed in beside it. This keeps the eight policies, the storage arm and the two functions in agreement.
- The Contestant App needs the exemption as a fact of the entity, beside the project setting, so that the exempt entity's pages show no note and accept edits.
- pgTAP: an exempt entity's own editor admitted on every site while the lock holds, a non-exempt one still refused, and an own editor unable to set its own exemption.
