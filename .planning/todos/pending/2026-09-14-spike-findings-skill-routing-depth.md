## Problem

`.claude/skills/README.md` asserts a one-level routing rule — "the body either contains the
instructions or points **directly** at concrete resource files — never at a second file whose own
body is predominantly pointers and which points onward". Phase 160 plan 09 shipped the instrument
that scores it, `.claude/scripts/audit-skill-routing.sh`, and the live corpus has **exactly one**
routing layer that fails it. Reproduce with:

```bash
bash .claude/scripts/audit-skill-routing.sh .claude/skills/spike-findings-voting-advice-application-gsd
```

Measured at commit `b90266ee7`, 2026-09-14. Five reported findings, each with its anchor:

| Finding | Anchor | Measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A2a — the routing layer carries two index sections, i.e. a second index inside the skill | `.claude/skills/spike-findings-voting-advice-application-gsd/SKILL.md:149` § _Feature Areas_ | 8 pointer lines of 9 body lines, **88.9%** |
| A2a — the second of them | `.claude/skills/spike-findings-voting-advice-application-gsd/SKILL.md:188` § _Production Landing Map_ | 26 of 36, **72.2%** — it re-indexes the same eight reference files through `[[wiki-alias]]` links |

The verdict does not depend on the threshold: re-run with `SKILL_ROUTING_THRESHOLD` at 25, 35, 43,
50, 60, 80, 95 or 100 and this layer is reported at every one of them, because the `## Related`
section driving it is at 100%.

**No other routing layer in the corpus fails.** `components`, `data`, `database`, `filters`,
`matching` and `ship-review-stack` all reach content in one hop.

## Why phase 160 did not fix it

Plan 09's own prohibition is explicit: flattening means **promoting or splitting, never
discarding** — the unique-synthesis removal cost `.claude/skills/README.md` § _Judgement 1_ records
applies to any deletion here exactly as it did to plan 07's. Both content-preserving shapes were
weighed and both are structural work outside a verification plan:

- **Promote** — fold the eight `references/*.md` digests into the `SKILL.md` body so the body IS the
  content and § _Feature Areas_ stops being an index. Cost: `SKILL.md` goes from **30,574 B** to
  about **116,000 B**, a single file roughly 3.5× the largest hand-authored skill in the tree
  (`ship-review-stack/SKILL.md`, 27,017 B) and about a third of the whole corpus. It trades a
  routing violation for a file nobody can read, against a corpus whose recorded verdict is that it
  is below the disclosure crossover precisely because it is navigable by direct reading.
- **Split** — make the eight reference files into eight skills with sharp, discriminative
  descriptions, so selection happens once at the description layer. Cost: eight new skill
  directories with eight hand-written descriptions, eight `targets:` lists, eight rows in
  `.claude/skills/BOUNDARIES.md`, and eight entries competing for selection in a corpus that
  currently has seven skills total. This is the option plan 09's own task-2 rule names as a filing
  case ("one over-large skill becoming several smaller ones").

**And both expire.** The skill is machine-generated by `/gsd-spike --wrap-up`.
`.claude/skills/README.md` records the objection against itself: "a hand-flatten is undone by the
next wrap-up run … the durable fix is to teach the generator rather than to re-edit the output."
That objection already stands against plan 07's hand edit to § _Source Files_; a second, much larger
hand edit compounds it.

## What to do

Teach the generator, not the output. `/gsd-spike --wrap-up` should emit a skill whose `SKILL.md`
body reaches its reference files in one hop and whose reference files carry no `## Related`
back-index — the cross-links belong in the body's single table, where they already are. Until then
the corpus carries one asserted rule with one recorded exception, which is the honest cost of plan
07's `delete-sources-only` outcome and is stated as such in `.claude/skills/README.md`.

**Do not resolve this by deleting `.claude/skills/spike-findings-voting-advice-application-gsd/references/`.**
Those eight files are 85,594 B of cross-spike synthesis — the migration inventory and wave order,
the consumer-migration codemod, the production landing map — that exists nowhere in
`.planning/spikes/` and, per the three measurements in `.claude/skills/README.md` § _Judgement 1_,
nowhere in `main`'s history either.
