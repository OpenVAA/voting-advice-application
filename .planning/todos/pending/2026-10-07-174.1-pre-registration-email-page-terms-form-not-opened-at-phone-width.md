## Problem

`TermsOfUseForm.svelte` has two consumers: the terms-of-use gate view, and the pre-registration email page (`routes/contestant/preregister/(authenticated)/email/+page.svelte`), which nests it as `<TermsOfUseForm bind:termsAccepted class="mt-md" />` inside `<form class="flex flex-col flex-nowrap items-center">`, after an intro `div` and two `input.input.mb-md.w-full.max-w-md`.

Phase 174.1 fixed the form's consent label, which could not wrap and put the checkbox left of a phone viewport. The gate view is held to 320 and 390 px by an E2E check and was read in a browser in seven locales. **The pre-registration page was never opened**, before the fix or after it.

Why not: the page sits behind the identity-provider flow. It needs the provider's `id_token` cookie and a project that allows the `identity_provider` registration mode; on the `e2e/base` dataset `GET /contestant/preregister/email` answers 303 to the login page (the research, 2026-10-07). The default suite sets none of that up, and no file of Phase 174.1 touches it.

What was measured instead, by plan 174.1-02 on 2026-10-07 on the fixed tree (session `174.1-02-reads`, head `947e45af6`, tree-equal to `1c5d2896d` outside `.planning/`): the page's nesting **rebuilt in the live gate page around the live form**, with the class lists read from the page's source at run time and its English intro message. English, Chromium, the dev server.

| Width | With the fix | With `whitespace-normal` taken off the label |
|-------|--------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| 320, phone descriptor | `scrollWidth` 320 = `clientWidth`; form 280 wide; checkbox at 20 … 44 | 448 against 320; form 576.11 wide; checkbox at −128.05 … −104.05 |
| 390, phone descriptor | 390 = 390; form 350; checkbox at 20 … 44 | 484 against 390; form 576.11; checkbox at −93.05 … −69.05 |
| 1280 | 1280 = 1280; form 576; checkbox at 352 | 1280 = 1280; form 576.11; checkbox at 351.95 |

So the nesting overflows exactly as the gate did without the class and fits with it, and it holds no second box that cannot shrink. Since the label is inside the shared component, the one-class fix reaches both consumers.

What that leaves open:

- It is a reconstruction. The real page's `MainContent` has no hero and a longer title, its inputs carry placeholders, and its primary action is another button; none of that is in the rebuilt nesting. Nothing known suggests a difference, and nothing measured rules one out.
- Only English was nested. The gate view was read in seven locales; the page's own strings (its title, its intro) were not laid out in any other.
- The page in a production build was not opened either.

## Solution

Open the real page once at 320 and 390 px and read `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` against `clientWidth`, and the rectangle of `[data-testid="terms-checkbox"]` against the viewport:

- when Phase 178 moves the form's second consumer into the rebuilt `register/` tree (the roadmap: "no `preregister/` route survives"), as part of that phase's own check of the new page; or
- sooner, in a `bank-auth-journey` run made for another reason, which already drives the identity-provider flow with the mock issuer.

If Phase 178 gives the new page a phone-width check of its own, that check closes this todo. If the page turns out to overflow, the cause is in the page's own nesting and not in the form: the form's label wraps.
