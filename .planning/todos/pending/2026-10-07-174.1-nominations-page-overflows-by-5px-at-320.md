## Problem

At a viewport 320 px wide the voter app's all-nominations page is 5 px wider than the viewport. The research of Phase 174.1 found it while checking other pages for the defect that phase fixed; it is a different defect.

What was measured, by the research, on 2026-10-07, on the tree before that phase's fix (`d6ad5ed92` outside `.planning/`), with the `e2e/base` dataset:

- The page: `/en/nominations`, as the research names it. (English is the base locale and is served without a prefix, so the same page is `/nominations`; which of the two forms the research opened is not recorded.)
- At 320 px the document overflows by 5 px. At 390 px it fits.
- The elements that end past the right edge belong to the entity cards, and their right edge is x = 325.2: the card's `header.gap-x-md.gap-y-xs.grid…`, the `div.gap-md.grid.items-start` and the `div.small-label.text-left` inside it. 50 elements end past the edge in all.
- No expander is among them (the page's one expander sits inside the closed filter dialog, 0 wide), and no DaisyUI `.label` either: the page has none outside that dialog. The cause Phase 174.1 fixed, a `.label` that cannot wrap, is not at work here.

In today's source the three class strings are in `EntityCard.svelte`: the `<header class="gap-x-md gap-y-xs grid items-center justify-items-start">`, and the `<div class="gap-md grid items-start">` with the inline `grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(8rem, 1fr))` that holds the `small-label text-left` cells.

What was not done:

- **Not re-measured by plan 174.1-02**, on the fixed tree or otherwise. The fix of that phase touches one label of the Contestant App and nothing of this page, so the figure is expected to stand, but that is a reading and not a measurement.
- Which element forces the width was not established. The research listed the elements that stick out, not the one that cannot shrink. In the terms-of-use view the widest elements were not the cause (the todo that reported it named two wrong candidates), so the three above are where to start and not a diagnosis.
- No other voter page was swept at 320 px, and no width between 320 and 390 was tried.

A 5 px overflow leaves everything reachable: the page scrolls sideways by 5 px. It is a reflow defect at the narrowest width WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.10 names, not a blocker.

## Solution

- Reproduce at 320 px and find the box that cannot shrink before touching anything: apply one override at a time to the candidates, as `174.1-RESEARCH.md` § M2 did for the terms-of-use view. The `minmax(8rem, 1fr)` tracks are the first to try: two of them with the card's gap and padding may not fit 320 px.
- Fix it in the component that forces the width, not by hiding overflow on an ancestor.
- A check that would hold it: the document's `scrollWidth` against `documentElement.clientWidth` at 320 px under the suite's phone descriptor, as `contestant-terms-gate-phone.spec.ts` asserts it. An axe scan does not see a horizontal overflow (`174.1-RESEARCH.md` § M10).
