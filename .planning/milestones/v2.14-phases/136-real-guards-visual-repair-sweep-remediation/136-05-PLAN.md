# Plan 136-05 — Visual regression repair (operator-authorised)

## Context — this is a repair, not a re-baseline

Established by inspection 2026-08-11:
- The four baselines depict **"Test Candidate Alpha"**. `test-candidate-alpha` is **not seeded by
  `e2e/base`** — it appears only in `tests/playwright.config.ts` comments and
  `tests/tests/utils/testCredentials.ts`.
- `auth.setup.ts` logs in as `TEST_CANDIDATE_EMAIL` (`mock.candidate.2@openvaa.org`), which has no
  registered account against base. `testCredentials.ts` documents this as a **deferred architectural
  item**: base candidates carry no email column, so the opt-in auth chain has nothing to log in as.
- The PNGs were generated on a developer Mac on 31 March, last touched in a **v1.2-era** commit
  (`05b033266`). There is **no `--update-snapshots` step anywhere in CI** and no commit-back — the
  "canonical CI runner re-baseline" referenced in the spec docblock does not exist as a process.
- `.github/workflows/main.yaml`'s `e2e-visual` job is `continue-on-error: true` and commented
  "currently expected to fail". Nothing consumes its result.

**Operator decision (2026-08-11): repair fully and make it blocking.**
