<objective>
Remove the `svelte/store` seam from the **data layer**: delete the hand-rolled `Readable<DataRoot>` bridge (`createDataRootBridge` + the `dataRootStore`/`dataRootExport` store shape) from `dataContext.svelte.ts`, drop the `Readable` member from `dataContext.type.ts`, and migrate the only two `get(dataRoot…)` consumers (`routes/+layout.svelte` + `routes/candidate/(protected)/+layout.svelte`) to the rune-native `reactiveDataRoot.instance` non-reactive handle.

This is an **atomic producer-drop + consumer-migration unit**: dropping the bridge export removes `dataRoot` as a store, so the two `get(dataRootStore)`/`get(dataRoot)` callsites MUST migrate in the same plan or the build goes red. The `reactiveDataRoot` handle (with its `current`/`instance` split) already exists in `dataContext.svelte.ts` — this plan deletes the redundant bridge and points the consumers at the existing `instance` getter.

Purpose: Close the data-layer half of CLEAN-01 (zero `svelte/store` in `lib/contexts/data/**` + the two layout consumers) while preserving DataRoot sequential-population semantics (Spike 002 — the `instance` handle is the documented replacement for the `get()` infinite-loop workaround).
Output: Rune-native `dataContext` with no store bridge; two layouts reading `reactiveDataRoot.instance`.
</objective>
