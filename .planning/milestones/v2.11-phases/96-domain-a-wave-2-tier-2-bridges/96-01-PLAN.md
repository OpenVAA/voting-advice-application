## Scope Decisions

These resolve the three open discretion calls (RESEARCH Q1–Q3) + the PATTERNS appContext Delta. Locked for this plan; CTX-06/07 acceptance is phrased to match.

- **appContext Delta (central to Plan B) → Option (ii), landed HERE in Plan A.** The shipped appContext exports `appSettings` (`appContext.svelte.ts:85`) and `locale` (`localeStore`, `:54,239`) as `toStore(...)` `Readable`s — NOT `.current` getters. Only `reactiveDataRoot` is a `.current` getter today (consumed at `voterContext.svelte.ts:56,98` without `fromStore`). CTX-07's literal requirement ("voterContext + candidateContext drop `fromStore(appSettings)`/`fromStore(locale)`") is therefore UNREACHABLE unless appContext also exposes runed inputs. Decision: **add `reactiveAppSettings` + `reactiveLocale` `.current` getters to appContext in Plan A** (additive — the `toStore` exports stay verbatim for un-migrated consumers; this mirrors the existing `reactiveDataRoot` precedent exactly). This is the least-scope-creep option that *actually satisfies* CTX-07 (the alternative — keeping `fromStore` — fails CTX-07). The two new getters wrap the SAME `$state` (`appSettingsValue` `:82`, `componentCtx.locale` `:54`) the `toStore` exports already wrap; no second source of truth.
- **Q3 survey/tracking bridge ownership → appContext seam (option b).** survey/tracking PRODUCERS become fully `svelte/store`-free (no `fromStore`, no `toStore`); the store-shaped EXPORTED surface that un-migrated consumers (`$surveyLink` in SurveyButton/VoterNav; `sendTrackingEventStore.set` + `$shouldTrack` in `routes/+layout.svelte:54,74,155`) still need is owned by appContext via `toStore(() => producer.current)`, mirroring the 9 bridges appContext already owns. This satisfies CTX-06's literal "no `fromStore`/`toStore` in survey / trackingService". The `trackingService.type.ts` `Readable`/`Writable` surface (`:39,43,47`) stays until Phase 98.
- **Q1 + Q2 (getRoute, preregistration) → resolved in Plan B (96-02).** Out of scope for this plan; noted there.
