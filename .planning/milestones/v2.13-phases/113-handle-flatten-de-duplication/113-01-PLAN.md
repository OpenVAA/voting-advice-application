codemod that rewrites `<handle>.current` → `<handle>` for EXACTLY the three in-scope handles

svelte-check stay at their exact current baselines after this plan.

Output: `apps/frontend/scripts/flatten-current-codemod.mjs`; extended `REACTIVE_ACCESSORS` set in
the spike-009 source; verified idempotency + zero-false-positive on the production tree (dry-run only).

    site. (The spike-009 source's own REACTIVE_ACCESSORS gets the same three names in Task 2; keep this
