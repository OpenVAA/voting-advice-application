<objective>
Convert the three derived-projection voter sub-stores — `matchStore`, `nominationAndQuestionStore`, and `filters/filterStore` — to Svelte 5 classes. All three share the identical `{ readonly value: T }` shape backed by `$derived.by`, take getter-args that close over their inputs, and are consumed ONLY by voterContext (via `.value`). Their exported TYPE aliases are re-imported widely and MUST keep their names/locations.

Purpose: CLASS-05 requires these sub-stores to be classes. They are independent of each other and of the orchestrator (each is a pure projection over getter-args), so they convert in parallel with Plan 01 and BEFORE the orchestrator (Plan 03), de-risking the HIGH-risk voterContext conversion.

Output: `matchStore.svelte.ts`, `nominationAndQuestionStore.svelte.ts`, `filterStore.svelte.ts` as classes with byte-identical factory signatures + `{ readonly value }` surfaces; the MatchTree/NominationAndQuestionTree/FilterTree type exports unchanged.
