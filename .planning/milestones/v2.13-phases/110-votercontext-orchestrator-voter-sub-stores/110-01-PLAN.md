<objective>
Convert the two LIVE voter/utils sub-stores `answerStore` and `paramStore` to Svelte 5 classes, and DELETE the three dead-code question projection factories (`questionCategoryStore`/`questionStore`/`questionBlockStore` `.svelte.ts`). This is the lowest-risk slice of CLASS-05 and de-risks the orchestrator conversion (Plan 03) by ensuring its sub-store dependencies are already class-shaped.

Purpose: CLASS-05 requires `voterContext` and its sub-stores to be classes while keeping the voter app behaving identically. `answerStore` (Group-C persisted) and `paramStore` (LIVE derived projection) are independent of the orchestrator and of each other, so they convert first. The three question projection factories were inlined into voterContext `$effect` blocks at Phase 61 and have ZERO live value-importers (grep-confirmed in 110-PATTERNS.md §3 + re-verified) — converting dead code is wasted risk against the SC-4 green gate, so they are deleted instead (per planning-context constraint 4: prefer DELETING confirmed-dead factory files over converting them, scoring SC-1 as satisfied-by-deletion).

Output: `answerStore.svelte.ts` and `paramStore.svelte.ts` as classes with byte-identical factory signatures + exported surfaces; three dead factory files removed; their live `.type.ts` sibling (`questionBlockStore.type.ts`) preserved.
</objective>
