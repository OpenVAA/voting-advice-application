# v2.14 E2E Workstream — Consolidated Discussion Points

**Purpose:** One place that pre-resolves every gray-area decision across the **E2E phases** of v2.14 —
the audit (118), fixtures+seed (119), the existing-feature spec builds (120 EPERM, 121 EFLOW, 122 bank-auth),
the deferred-build new-feature cluster (129 build, 130 specs), and the reliability close (131 flake triage,
132 green gate). Fill it once and `/gsd-plan-phase` + `/gsd-execute-phase` can run **autonomously** across the
whole E2E workstream without a per-phase discuss step.

> Scope note: RUNES (123-124) and TYPE/svelte-check (125-128) are **separate, independent workstreams** and are
> NOT in this doc. This doc is the batched stand-in for `/gsd-discuss-phase` across the E2E phases only.

**How to read / fill:**
- Each item is a decision with a **recommended default** (`→`). That default is what I execute autonomously
  unless you change it.
- `[ ]` = pending your review · `[x]` = you confirm the default · strike/edit the `→` line to override.
- **⚠ DECIDE** marks items that genuinely want your call before I lock them — they change the *shape* of the
  work, not just an implementation detail. Skim for these first.
- Bare `[ ]` with no ⚠ are "confirm-or-ignore": leaving them unchecked still means I proceed on the default.

**Grounding:** `.planning/ROADMAP.md` (v2.14 Phases 118-132 detail), `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`
(EPERM/EFLOW/EQTYP/UNBLK/HARDN + per-requirement NOTEs + operator-mandated E2E ordering), v2.10 final suite
(82/2) and v2.11 gate (84/0) as the determinism-standard precedent.
