## Reconciliations (where two selections interact)

- **R1 — 102-1 ⇄ 102-2 (read-write handle idiom vs "zero `.current` everywhere"):** The **north star is zero `.current` everywhere** (102-2, max-native on *both* classes). The chosen read-write surface (102-1, get/set accessor pair) is therefore applied **at the context-property level** — `get x()` / `set x(v)` on the context object, so consumers read `ctx.x` and write `ctx.x = v` — **eliminating** the nested `.current` rather than retaining it. Phase 102's spike validates per-handle feasibility on the PoC and **documents (does not force)** any handle that genuinely must keep a handle shape (allowed per REQUIREMENTS Out-of-Scope). The spike's decision record finalizes this; the north star is zero `.current`.
- **R2 — DX-5 ⇄ 103-1 (pause-for-review vs auto-apply):** The codemod is **auto-applied and committed in one shot** (not interactive/batched), correctness-gated by `typecheck` + `lint` + **full E2E** (103-1) — no pre-commit blocking human review. The DX-5 "Phase 103 codemod diff review" is satisfied **post-apply**: the committed diff is reviewed at the per-phase PR (DX-3), and the autonomous chain surfaces the diff + result before starting Phase 104. A **single E2E run** is used as cheap mid-chain validation right after the codemod lands (see K3).
