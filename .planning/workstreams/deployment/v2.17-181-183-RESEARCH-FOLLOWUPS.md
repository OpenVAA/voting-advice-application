- [ ] ★ RECOMMENDED — Once per view, at the first of: a client navigation, the tab becoming hidden, or
      `pagehide` as a fallback. Time after a return to the tab is not measured.
- [ ] At page leave only, as I first read it. Whole durations; landing-only visits on mobile are often lost.
- [ ] Two sends joined by a random token that lives for one page view. Reliable counts and whole durations; it
      is an identifier, which conflicts with "no id".
- [ ] No duration. The pageview is sent on arrival. Reverses your note.

### 182-R2 — The opt-out checkbox in the Contestant app ⚠ DECIDE

Acceptance is stored per user on the server; the consent choice is stored per browser.

- [ ] ★ RECOMMENDED — Unticked by default, labelled "do not collect usage data". Pre-ticked when the browser
      already holds a refusal from the voter popup, so acceptance never flips it. Hidden when `Sec-GPC` is set.
      A contestant who accepted on another device grants nothing here, and only the pageview is sent.
- [ ] A ticked-by-default "allow usage data" box. Pre-ticked consent boxes were ruled invalid under GDPR by the
      EU Court of Justice in Planet49 (2019).
- [ ] The choice is stored with the acceptance on the server, per user. Follows the user across devices; it is
      a schema change that must also land before the freeze.
