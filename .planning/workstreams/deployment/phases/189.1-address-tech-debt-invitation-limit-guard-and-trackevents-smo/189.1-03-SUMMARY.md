## Accomplishments

- Step `id: settings`, named "Check the deployment settings", sits directly after `id: migrations` and before `id: functions`. Its env is exactly `REF`, `PGHOST` (from `vars.SUPABASE_POOLER_HOST`), `PGPORT: "5432"`, `PGDATABASE: postgres`, `PGCONNECT_TIMEOUT: "10"` and `PGPASSWORD`. It has no access token, no AWS credential and no `${{ }}` in `run:`. It checks the pooler host is set, exports `PGUSER="postgres.$REF"`, reads the status as `settings_exit=0` then `|| settings_exit=$?`, prints `settings_exit=<n>`, and on a non-zero status prints `::error::check-deployment-settings.sql exited <n>.` and exits 1.
- The comment above the step covers three things: why the setting matters, that only the operator's bootstrap writes it, and how the prefix tells a finding from a check that could not run.
- The alert reads `SETTINGS_OUTCOME` and has the pair `"settings:$SETTINGS_OUTCOME"` between the migrations and functions pairs. Header item 2 now names the check between the migrations push and the functions deploy.
- `deploy-workflow.test.ts` changes:
  - `settings` is added to `STAGE_ORDER` and `WITHOUT`.
  - The secret holders are now link, migrations, settings and functions, with 7 mentions.
  - `stageOf('settings')` is now `settings`.
  - A new `SETTINGS_ENV` constant and `settingsEnvHolds` helper.
  - Four new cases.
- In `infra/README.md`, § The gated job and its order has item 4 **Settings check.**, with the later items renumbered through 10 **Failure alert.** § Reading a red run defines `settings` in the stage sentence and adds a `settings` table row after `migrations`. The row covers four cases: status 3 with the prefix is a finding, fixed by Step 20 with `BOOTSTRAP_BEHIND_CLOUDFLARE=true`; status 3 with any other error means the check could not run; status 2 is a lost connection; and the run's migrations are already pushed.
