<objective>
See the budget alert fire once at a test amount, confirmed by the operator from their mailbox, then raise the budget to its standing 100 USD.

Purpose: INFRA-15 and criterion 3 ("a budget on the account names a threshold and notifies the operator's address, and the operator confirms that the notification reaches them"). An alert counts once it has been seen firing.

Blocked on: plan 183-16, because the budget exists from its apply, and plan 183-17, because the hosted zone that apply creates may be the only spend in a quiet account. It does not wait for the delegation at Cloudflare. It runs in the same wave as the delegation, the mail-identity wait and the start of the Supabase work, so its own wait holds none of them.

The wait is a checkpoint, not a sleep: this plan pauses at Task 1 until the operator has the mail or reports that none can come.

What limits the test, as AWS documents it and as this plan must not overstate:
- Budget data is updated up to three times a day, so the mail is not immediate and can take a day.
- An actual alert fires when month-to-date cost passes 80 percent of the limit, and is sent once per budget period.
- A forecast alert is not triggered until about five weeks of usage data exist. It is configured here and will not be seen in this phase.

The cases, each with its route:
- The budget's computed spend is 0.80 USD or more: the 1 USD budget fires as it is.
- It is between 0.01 and 0.80: the budget is re-applied at a test amount just under the spend divided by 0.8, never below 0.01 USD. Whether AWS accepts so small a budget is unobserved; if it refuses, the operator pastes the error and the plan stops for a revision.
- It is below 0.01, or the account runs on credits and shows no spend: no cost budget alert can fire, because an alert needs spend above its threshold. The budget already excludes credits, so usage should show as spend even on a credits account; if it does not, the plan has no way to make an alert fire. The checkpoint then ends with the outcome "no alert can fire yet", the evidence records the spend and the date, and INFRA-15 stays open until spend appears. That is a stated outcome of this plan, not a pass.
- If the lock was not seen in plan 183-16, Task 1 step 4 observes it during the raise.

Open follow-up this plan rests on: 183-R2 (the alert address is given per session and never tracked). Plan 183-14 recorded the ruling as a line `183-R2: per-session` or `183-R2: tracked`. If it is `tracked`, the address is in the bootstrap root's variable file by the operator's choice: Task 1's check that the file holds no `@` is then skipped, and the export of `TF_VAR_alert_email` is left out.
Output: a dated evidence file and an updated runbook.

Decisions implemented: D-11 (seen firing at a test amount, then raised to 100 USD a month), A-03 (only the actual alert, only above 80 percent of spend, the mail can take a day, the address untracked).
</objective>
