The wire shape this plan fixes. The browser posts one JSON body per flush:
- envelope: `{ routeId, locale, events }`. `routeId` is the route id of the view the events belong to; `locale` is the locale of that view.
- every event: `{ name, ago, attributes }`, and `id` where the event may carry the visit id. `ago` is the event's age in whole milliseconds at send time; the relay computes the event time as its receive time minus `ago`.
- the pageview: `{ name: 'pageview', ago, attributes: { params, duration } }`. It has no `id` member. Its route id and locale are the envelope's.

The pageview's `duration` member rests on the recommended answer of follow-up 182-R1 in `v2.17-181-183-RESEARCH-FOLLOWUPS.md` (one send per view, at the first of a client navigation, the tab becoming hidden, or `pagehide`). The operator has not ruled on it. What an overrule changes: with "no duration, sent on arrival" the member is removed here and from plans 182-08, 182-13 and 182-15; with "two sends joined by a token that lives for one page view" the pageview gains a token member and those three plans are revised; with "at page leave only" this schema stays as it is and only the send points of plans 182-13, 182-15 and 182-26 change.
