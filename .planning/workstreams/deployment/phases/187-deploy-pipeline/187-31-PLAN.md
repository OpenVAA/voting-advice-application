**The fix (Claude's discretion, recorded).** The step reads the service in a loop. Every condition C-06 set stays: the primary deployment runs the task definition the apply wrote, its rollout is `COMPLETED`, and at least `task_floor` tasks run.
- `IN_PROGRESS` on the applied task definition is the only state that is read again, after `sleep 15`, up to 60 reads.
- Any other outcome decides at once: the existing error line and exit 1 for another task definition, `FAILED`, any other state, or a completed rollout below the floor; exit 0 for a completed rollout at the floor.
- At the 60th read still `IN_PROGRESS`, the step prints its own error line and exits 1.
- A failed `describe-services` ends the step at once, as now.
- Each read prints `stable_poll=<n>` and the five lines the step prints now, so a red run shows every state it saw.
- Statuses are captured as `name=0` then `cmd || name=$?`, as plan 187-29 established. Nothing turns errexit off, swallows a status or adds a step-level tolerance key.

**The bound: 60 reads, 15 s apart, about 15 minutes (Claude's discretion, recorded).**
- *Observed lag.* The deployment went `COMPLETED` 25 to 32 s after Terraform's wait returned: two or three reads at this interval.
- *ECS's circuit breaker.* With one desired task its threshold is three failed tasks (50 % of the desired count, at least 3, per the ECS documentation). A task of this service fails either at once, when it exits, or after its start, the 60 s health-check grace period and three failed 15 s container checks. On staging, on 2026-11-13, the breaker failed a rollout whose tasks exited at once in about seven minutes, and the apply exited 1 with its message (staging AWS runbook, Step 10). A rollout the stable step still finds `IN_PROGRESS` is one the breaker can still fail or complete. About twice the measured trip lets the step report the breaker's own `FAILED`, with its stage, rather than its own limit.
- *The job's 60-minute timeout.* Attempt 3's whole deploy job took 5.5 minutes without a build, and attempt 2's build took 181 s. The poll's worst case leaves more than half the job for the build, the migrations, the apply, the Playwright install and the smoke run. The rule `rollout-poll` holds the limit to at least 600 s and at most half the job's `timeout-minutes`.
- *The role session.* The stable step uses the credentials assumed right before the apply, which last an hour. The apply (112 s in attempt 3) plus 15 minutes stays well inside it.
- *Interval.* 15 s is the AWS CLI's own `ecs wait services-stable` interval. One read per 15 s is far below the API's limits, and the log gets at most 60 reads of six lines.
- *Not chosen: `aws ecs wait services-stable`.* Its success condition is one deployment with running equal to desired, which attempt 3's single read already saw. It never looks at `rolloutState`, and at its own limit it ends the step with the CLI's error and none of the step's lines.
- *Not chosen: a wall-clock bound.* A count of reads with a fixed sleep can be tested under a stub `sleep` without waiting, and bounds the step's time as well: each read adds about a second.
