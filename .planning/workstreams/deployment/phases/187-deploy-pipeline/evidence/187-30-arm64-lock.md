- In `git archive HEAD infra` under `tmp/187-30/locks/t2/` (HEAD `f62faf522`), for each root but `infra/envs/staging/aws`, one after another (`tmp/187-30/locks/t2-run.sh`): `init -backend=false -input=false -lockfile=readonly -no-color`, then `providers lock -platform=linux_amd64 -platform=linux_arm64 -platform=darwin_arm64 -platform=darwin_amd64`, each status 0 (`tmp/187-30/locks/t2-logs/`). Each lock log printed `Additional checksums for this platform are now tracked in the lock file` for linux_arm64 only, and `All checksums for this platform were already tracked` for the other three platforms. Each lock was `cp`'d back to its root; none was edited by hand.
- **The added hashes.** Every lock gained exactly one `h1:` line per provider, and no hash was removed: `hashicorp/aws` 6.68.0 `h1:s5EXu9EtGfnLR3ebHoIyZBLRYMPcRL9zcfx/NKi+Q9o=` in its five locks, `hashicorp/random` 3.9.1 `h1:nozfr4CZq73d4HjubKJundX/8A+Mj282oS/hyNfjUPU=` in its two, and `supabase/supabase` 1.11.0 `h1:CJzPqGBmaFDS1gTr1TS3IaRe9WEupi9CciEuUXGWGj8=` in its three. No `version` line changed.
- **Against the plan: the three Supabase locks also rewrote their `constraints` line**, from `"~> 1.11"` to `"1.11.0, ~> 1.11"`. That is the `removed=1` and the second `added` line of those three. `providers lock` records the constraints the configuration states today: each Supabase root pins `version = "= 1.11.0"` (commit `39df97fbd`, which came after these locks were written) and `infra/modules/supabase` asks `~> 1.11`, so the recorded line had been stale since that pin, and `init -lockfile=readonly` does not rewrite it. The plan's assumption that every lock diff only adds `h1:` lines did not hold for these three. The line was not put back by hand, which the plan forbids; `LOCKS_ADDED_ONE_H1_PER_PROVIDER` and `LOCK_VERSIONS_UNCHANGED`, whose conditions include no removed line and no changed `constraints` line, are therefore recorded `no`, and the four lines after them say what does hold. The selected version is 1.11.0 before and after, so no init selects another package; the runs below show every root initialising with `-lockfile=readonly` and no warning on the new locks, on darwin_arm64 and on linux_arm64.
