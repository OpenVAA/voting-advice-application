- **The prompt is the one modal dialog.** `dialog:modal` matched once in each variant, and that element's `aria-label` is `Collecting Usage Data`. Its box is the whole viewport in each variant: the `dialog` element itself spans it.
- **The last answer choice cannot take focus under it.** After `focus()` on the fifth `question-choice`, `document.activeElement` was a button inside the dialog, not the choice. The same was asked of every `a[href]`, `button`, `input`, `select`, `textarea` and `[tabindex]` element outside the dialog, 33 of them in each variant, and none took focus.
- **A pointer at the choice's label lands in the dialog.** The label's centre was inside the viewport in each variant, at `scrollY` 0. `document.elementFromPoint` there returned an element whose closest `dialog` is the modal one: a button inside the dialog at 1280x720 in both schemes, so the prompt's own content lies over that point, and a `div` inside the dialog at 390x844. The label and input boxes at 1280x720 are the ones 189.1-08 measured.
- **Escape closes the prompt and stores `denied`.** Half a second after the measured Escape, the prompt's count by role and name was 0, no `dialog:modal` and no `dialog[open]` was left, and the stored preferences held `dataCollection.consent` `denied`. The first Escape, on the home page, was followed by the hard `toBeHidden` on the prompt, which passed in each variant.
- **Afterwards the choice takes focus with nothing over it.** `focus()` then made the choice `document.activeElement`, and the hit test at the label's centre returned the label itself, outside any dialog.
- **The dark scheme was in effect.** In the third variant `matchMedia('(prefers-color-scheme: dark)')` matched and the body's computed background was `rgb(0, 0, 0)`, against `rgb(255, 255, 255)` in the other two.
- **`A11Y_FINDING=none`, in each variant.** No focusable control lies under the prompt, since none outside the dialog can take focus while it is open, and after Escape the choice is reachable and uncovered. Against the before-state of `evidence/189.1-08-prompt-on.md`, measured on build `2ddc331d1` at 1280x720: there the last choice took focus under the prompt (`A11Y_FOCUSED_IS_LAST_CHOICE=yes`), its label lay inside the non-modal dialog's box in full, Escape did not close the prompt (`A11Y_ESCAPE_CLOSES=no`), and the finding was `focus-entirely-obscured`.
