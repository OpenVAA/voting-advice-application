    after one Escape the prompt alone is open and no choice is stored, in all three runs. Why the tree already gets this right: both modals listen for the key on `document` (`<svelte:document onkeydown={handleEscape} />` in each), and Svelte 5's event wrapper (`create_event` in svelte 5.57.1, `if (!event.cancelBubble)`) does not call a later handler on the same node once `cancelBubble` is set. DrawerHost mounts with the voter layout, before the prompt's ModalContainer, so its handler runs first and its `e.stopPropagation()` keeps the prompt's handler from running. The browser's own Escape handling then sends `cancel` to the topmost dialog, the drawer, whose `oncancel` cancels it. The outcome rests on listener order and on the drawer being topmost, not on the drawer cancelling the key's default action
