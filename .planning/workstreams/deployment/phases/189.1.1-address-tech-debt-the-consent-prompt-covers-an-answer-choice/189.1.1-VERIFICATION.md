## Why the status changed

Plan 189.1.1-02 states as a must-have: with a modal opened inside another, one Escape closes only the inner one and does not run the outer's `onClose`, "so the consent prompt of plan 06 cannot be declined by an Escape meant for its nested information dialog (D-01, research Pitfall 1)". That is part of what the phase promised: RELAY-22 asks that the prompt's dismissals decline it "as its decline control does", and a decline the voter did not choose is the case the nested guard exists to prevent. The roadmap criterion 1 does not name the nested case, but the plan truth, D-01 and the research pitfall all do, and the user-facing consequence (a stored decline that keeps the prompt from reappearing) is a consent-recording error. I count it as a must-have.

My first pass marked this truth VERIFIED from the jsdom unit cases (which I ran: they pass). That was a behavior-dependent truth (a cancellation and ordering invariant) accepted on a test that cannot exercise the platform step that breaks it. By this agent's own rule it should have been PRESENT_BEHAVIOR_UNVERIFIED at best. The code reading now settles it as FAILED:

- `ModalContainer.svelte` lines 96-103: `handleClose(); e.stopPropagation();` and no `e.preventDefault()`. I read it directly.
- I re-ran the staging script (`node staging-nested-escape.mjs <repo root>`) and got `OPEN_BEFORE=1`, `OPEN_WITH_INFO=2`, `OPEN_AFTER_ONE_ESCAPE=0`, and `appContext-userPreferences` holding `"consent":"denied"`.
- `189.1.1-REVIEW.md` CR-01 and WR-01 reproduce it in three engines and name the one-line fix.
