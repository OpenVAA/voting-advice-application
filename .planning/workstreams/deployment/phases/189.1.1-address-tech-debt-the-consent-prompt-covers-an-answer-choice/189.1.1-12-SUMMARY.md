- **Nine key paths differ, and they are one rename.** Five paths of the old row are absent (`access.answersLocked`, `contestantApp.entityTypes`, `contestantApp.registrationModes` and its `candidate` and `organization`); four are new (`access.contestantEditing`, `contestantApp.servedEntityTypes`, its `candidate`, and that one's `registrationModes`). - **One hand-set value was not restored: the candidate registration mode `identity_provider`.** The old row held it beside the template's `email` and `code`; the rebuilt row holds `email` and `code` only. The sitting restored `analytics.trackEvents` and nothing else, as D-16 orders: any key other than `analytics.trackEvents` is restored only if the operator chooses to. ## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] `infra/scripts/bootstrap.sql` failed whenever `BOOTSTRAP_ACCESS_CALLER_MAX` was unset**
- **Found during:** Task 3, run 1 of the sitting, Step 9
- **Issue:** the `access_caller_max` statement held `btrim('')::integer` in its `SET` list. Postgres evaluates that constant when it plans the statement, so the cast raised although the `WHERE` was false, and the script exited 3. It is one transaction, so nothing was written.
- **Fix:** `NULLIF(btrim(...), '')::integer`, by the orchestrator at top level. Checked on the local stack with the transaction rolled back: unset keeps 30, `50` is written, `abc` is refused before any write.
- **Files modified:** infra/scripts/bootstrap.sql
- **Commit:** ed35c98ec
- **Left open:** no test runs `bootstrap.sql`, so nothing guards the fix. Recorded in `deferred-items.md` (`441fbfb23`).

- **The sitting was one script.** `evidence/189.1.1-12-rebuild-sitting.sh` (`0e2c541f9`, then `018b144d2`) runs the written steps, checks each step's named lines, goes on by itself on a match, prints the failing command's error line on a stop, and resumes with `--from N`.
