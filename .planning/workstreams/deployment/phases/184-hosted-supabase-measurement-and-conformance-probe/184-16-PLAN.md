<interfaces>
`account-access` `request-link` body: `{"operation":"request-link","project_id":"<uuid>","email":"<address>","locale":"en","witness":"<uuid>"}`; it always answers 200 `{"accepted":true}` and works in the background. Its log lines begin `account-access:`; a failed send logs `a link could not be issued or mailed; its slot is given back` with the failure's name and code. `public.invitation_link_witness_open()` returns a witness id; `public.invitation_link_witness(<id>)` returns the outcome (`sent` or `not-sent`). The throttle rows are `private.invitation_throttle` keyed `link:<project>:<hash>`. A positive sign-up check is remembered per worker for 60 s and a positive redirect check for 10 minutes, so each trial redeploys `account-access` to start from fresh workers.
</interfaces>
</context>
