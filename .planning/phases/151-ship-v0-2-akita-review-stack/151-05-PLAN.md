## The tradeoff being chosen, not inherited

**459 code files are touched by both the pre-v2.4 prefix and the post-v2.4 tail** (re-measured in
plan 151-04; 3,598 prefix files, 901 tail files, 459 in both, 3,139 prefix-only, 442 tail-only).

D-09 locks the split axis: the 35-commit pre-v2.4 prefix splits **chronologically**, the 891-file
post-v2.4 tail splits **by subsystem**. That decision is not re-opened here. What the 459 number
forces is a choice about *how* the chronological framing is expressed:

- A literally chronological prefix PR shows the reviewer the v1.2 version of 459 files that a later
  tail PR rewrites — which is exactly what criterion 4.4.1 forbids at the commit level and what the
  root `ROADMAP.md` Addendum says to "merge or split away".
- **Adopted (research recommendation, Q1): path-partition *within* the chronological framing.** The
  chronological axis is expressed by the *position* of slices 01a and 01b at the bottom of the stack
  — they are the monorepo layout move and the Strapi removal, the chronologically first structural
  events, and they are pure renames and pure deletions respectively. Every other file, prefix-only
  or overlapping, lands in the subsystem slice that owns its path, where its **final state** lives.
  The reviewer sees the end state of early work, never a version a later PR undoes.

This is surfaced so the operator sees the tradeoff was chosen. It is not a change to D-09.

## Discretion calls made (flagged, not decisions changed)

- **Q4 — PR #1 is split into 01a and 01b.** As proven in research, a single PR #1 is 1,565 files:
  1,316 pure renames costing 0 lines, plus 249 deletions costing 46,188 lines. D-11's stated purpose
  is that "paths change and contents don't"; 46,188 deleted lines are contents changing. Splitting
  gives 01a a perfectly clean zero-line rename list — the entire point of D-11 — and 01b a
  self-evident "delete the dead backend" PR. Within "mechanics of reconstructing the pure-rename
  commit" (Claude's Discretion). Costs one PR of the 8–12 budget.
- **C-3 — the rename rule covers `docs/` → `apps/docs/` as well as `frontend/` → `apps/frontend/`.**
  271 files. Omitting them would render those files as delete/add pairs in a later PR, which is
  precisely what D-11 exists to prevent.
- **Frontend is sub-split into lib / routes+params / messages.** The 526-file `src/lib` surface is
  the largest single reviewing surface in the stack; `messages/` is 47 files × 7 locales of
  identical shape and reads as one mechanical thing. Splitting is within criterion 6's own
  instruction and Pitfall 8's rendering budget.
