Harden the diagnostic assertion, then run the zero-cost half of the hunt: force the failure with the
budget lever alone and test the View-Transition hypothesis with a media emulation that requires no
app change at all.

Implements D-08 (the soft→hard promotion, which CONTEXT.md makes unconditional — it happens
regardless of what the diagnosis finds) and the first half of D-01's forcing harness. RESEARCH §R2.4

cheapest, highest-information experiment available and it cannot itself be the cause: the app's own
animation gate already short-circuits on `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, so emulating that media
query switches the View Transition off without touching a line of app code.

Purpose: criterion 1 requires the failure to happen ON DEMAND before any fix is written. This plan
either delivers that with the cheap lever or delivers the recorded evidence that the cheap lever is
insufficient — which is itself a finding about the mechanism and redirects plan 03.

Output: the promoted assertion in `voter-journey.spec.ts`, and `138-FORCED-REPRO.md` carrying the
sweep table, the tri-state at every failure, the non-degeneracy verdict, and the A/B result.
