<objective>
Produce the evidence that UNIT-03 actually asserts — a committed, per-package pass/fail record whose
commit **predates** every wiring commit — and capture the three "before" halves the standing acceptance
rule demands while the tree is still unwired and the halves are still capturable.

This plan ships **zero product code**. Every file it touches under `packages/` is transient: created,
run, recorded, and removed inside the same task. That is deliberate: D-14 makes measure-before-wire a
constraint on plan *shape*, so the measurement must be its own plan in its own wave, and the wiring
must be structurally unable to precede it.

Purpose: after this plan, "we checked the tests were green before wiring them" is a git-ancestry fact
rather than a claim, and the three blindness observations (UNIT-01, UNIT-02, UNIT-04) exist as runs
rather than as predictions. Once plan 02 lands, none of them can be captured again.

Output: `141-MEASUREMENT.md` (the UNIT-03 record) and `141-NEGATIVE-CONTROL.md` (the phase's two-run
ledger, opened here and appended to by plans 02 and 03).

**No tracer task in this plan.** Tracer-first applies to the first plan that changes behaviour; this
plan modifies no layer of the system — it is the ordering prerequisite D-14 imposes on the plan that
does. Plan 02 leads with the tracer.
</objective>
