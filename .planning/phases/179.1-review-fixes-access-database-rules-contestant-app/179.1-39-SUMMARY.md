## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The fixture reloads after each change of colour scheme**
- **Found during:** Task 1, before the first run, reading `tests/tests/utils/axeScan.ts`.
- **Issue:** the plan reads both schemes through `page.emulateMedia` alone. That file records a measurement that a flip on a drawn page leaves some elements' computed colours in the previous scheme, which could make the dark half prove nothing. I did not re-measure that here.
- **Fix:** `inEachColourScheme` reloads after a change of scheme and ends on the scheme it started on.
- **Commit:** `fb1412bdc`.

**2. [Rule 2 - Missing check] The dark reading is proved to be dark**
- `expectClosedEntryIconDimmed` also requires the label's colour to differ between the schemes. Without this, a dark reading stuck on light colours would pass.

**5. Three unit cases have no red without the fix**
