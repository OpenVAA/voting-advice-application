# Phase 179.1 Plan 88: The start and end announcements of a Log out Summary

**A Log out now posts `start` before its requests and `end` once they settle, and another tab names it as the cause while a `start` is outstanding (up to 30 s) or within 5 s of the `end`. The Admin App's sign-out of a non-administrator posts the same pair, and the comments keep "announcement" for the channel message alone.**

## For the operator's reading

Say a user presses Log out in one tab and its requests take longer than 5 seconds, up to 30. A tab that follows that sign-out still says "You have been logged out in another tab or window." Before this plan it said nothing once the requests took more than 5 s, which the WR16J UAT reproduced at 7 s. When the Admin App signs out a user who is no administrator, that user's other tabs now say the same sentence as well. No string, key or screen changes.

What stays unproven (rule 17):
- A Log out slower than 30 seconds. Its other tabs reach the login page with no reason. Only a unit case covers the cap.
- The Admin App's case in a browser. A unit case covers it, but no spec drives a non-administrator's sign-out with a second tab open.
- Safari, Firefox and phones. The runs are Chromium; `BroadcastChannel` in the others is not observed by this plan.
