## For the operator's reading

**What a user now meets.** Log out with an unsaved edit on the profile, a question or the nomination details opens the app's modal and raises no native question. Stay (Continue, the close button or Escape) keeps the session, the edit and its draft. The modal's Log out or the count's end signs out and discards the edit, so a person who walks away is signed out and their edit is gone, **when the sign-out succeeds**. This is your ruling 1. `T-179.1-89` is retired and `T-179.1-98` registered. Ordinary link navigation keeps the native question.

**The limit of that.** A sign-out that fails at the count's end leaves that person signed in with their draft in storage. Plan 45 makes this visible and registers it as `T-179.1-104`. In this plan `#authLogout` still resolves on a failure, so this state begins in plan 45.

**Stay during a sign-out.** Continue is disabled and the modal's Log out shows its loading state while a sign-out runs. The close button and Escape stay live in that window. A user who presses one may believe they stayed, yet on a successful sign-out they are signed out and the edit is gone. Plan 46 records it in `deferred-items.md` and on the UAT list.
