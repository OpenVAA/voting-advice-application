## For the operator's reading

**`T-179.1-104`, which limits ruling 1 and is yours to overrule.** A user who walks away and whose sign-out fails at the count's end stays signed in, their draft stays in storage, and the message is on the screen for whoever comes next. Nothing retries by itself. Test 9 pins this state. So ruling 1's "nothing left on a shared computer" holds when the sign-out succeeds, and ruling 2 keeps the edits when it does not. Both rulings are honoured as written, and this is what they leave together. It is registered as accepted, and goes on plan 46's UAT list.
