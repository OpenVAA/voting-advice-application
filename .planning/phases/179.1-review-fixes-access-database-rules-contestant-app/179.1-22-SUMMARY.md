# Phase 179.1 Plan 22: the home as one list of what there is to do Summary

**On the logged-in home, an address awaiting its link is now the task list's first row: a link with a mail icon, naming the address, to the settings page. The reminder above the title and its Send again are gone. The preview entry is the list's last item, every entry that cannot be entered leads with the lock, and the list keeps the width of the small container through the new `.soft-min-w-sm`. A user who edits several profiles has Switch profile with the sync icon at the top of the home, and the menu's switch item uses the same icon. Along the way, the terms-of-use view's open step was made to wait for hydration, which removed the race that reddened the first green control.**

## The terms-of-use race (checkpoint resolution)

The first green control `179.1-22-green` was red outside its project: `contestant-terms-gate-phone` (Continue stayed disabled) and `contestant-journey` (90 s timeout under load). The user chose to fix the race and rerun when the host was calm.

- **What the failing trace shows.** Observed: the box was ticked at 0.81 s, the enabled check started at 0.83 s with its 5 s budget and failed at 5.84 s, and the first icon module was requested at 5.83 s. Inferred from that: since a page requests its icons only after it has mounted, the page hydrated about 5 s after the tick, on a host whose 5-minute load was 17.
- **The tick is not lost (observed).** With the client entry module held for 3 s and no fix (`179.1-22-terms-race-3s-red`), both widths passed: Continue enabled once the page hydrated. The defect is the enabled check's 5 s budget racing a slow hydration, not a lost tick.
- **Red and green in isolation (observed).** An uncommitted probe in the spec's composition root held `**/generated/client/app.js*` until 7 s after the box was ticked, or 4 s when nothing ticked it first. Without the wait both widths were red on `toBeEnabled()` (`179.1-22-terms-race-held-red`, verdict `--red` PASS); with it both passed (`179.1-22-terms-race-held-green`, `--green --were-red-in` PASS). The probe was never committed.
- **Earlier probes, kept.** `179.1-22-terms-race-red` (fixed 7 s hold, no wait) was red on both widths. `179.1-22-terms-race-green` (fixed 7 s hold, with the wait) had one width green and one out of the wait's 10 s budget during a host-load spike of 37 (macOS `duetexpertd`, `mobileassetd`, `mds`), and its stack restore failed (`supabase_analytics` did not start); the fixed hold left the wait only about 3 s. The stack was restarted with `yarn db:stop` and `yarn db:start` once both loads were under 10 (`edge_runtime` Up), and the probe was redesigned to hold relative to the tick.
- **The journey timeout** has no tree fix and was not touched; the spec and its 90 s budget are unchanged. `179.1-22-green-r2` passed it on the calmer host.
