1. **The callback redirects before the mails change** (execution rules PL-07), so at no commit is a mailed link unverifiable.
2. **The population of openers is derived at run time**: `git grep -n -E "(signInLinkToOpen|recoveryLinkToOpen|confirmationLinkToOpen)\(" -- tests/tests` and every `page.goto` or `context` navigation whose argument is such a link; on the planning day it is `passwordless-session.setup.ts`, `identityProviderFlow.fixture.ts` and the specs of `files_modified`.
