## Rulings carried by this plan

1. **In `Button`, one constant** (orchestrator): the three group variants move into `DISABLED_TEXT_CLASS`, read by `labelClass` and given to `Icon` as `class`. Not on the wrapping `div`, which also holds `Loading` and the badge.
2. **The contrast is what the operator asked for** (orchestrator). The drawn lock measures about 1.45:1 in light and 1.44:1 in dark. WCAG 2.1 1.4.11 does not require contrast of an inactive component (read from a search digest of the Understanding page, not the page itself), and the closed state is also carried by the reason sentence, at 5.74:1 and 6.25:1, and by `aria-disabled`. The axe scans stay green: axe does not read an icon's fill. An SVG fill takes no text shadow, so in dark the lock is fainter than the label, which `.btn`'s shadow lifts to 2.26:1.
