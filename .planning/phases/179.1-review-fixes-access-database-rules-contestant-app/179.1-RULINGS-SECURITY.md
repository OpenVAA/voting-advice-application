# Phase 179.1: security audit of the rulings' gap-closure plans

Verdict: **SECURED** at ASVS 1. All 15 threats of these registers are closed: T-179.1-97 to -110, plus T-179.1-SC, which all
four plans repeat. Ten are verified mitigations and five are accepted risks. Of the earlier rows these plans change,
T-179.1-87, -88, -90, -91 and -92 hold as restated, and T-179.1-89 is retired.

Three findings need the operator's attention. The UAT list states none of these facts.

1. **The logout modal can close without the component being told, and its count then runs on unseen.** At its end the user
   is signed out and the edit is gone, after they dismissed the modal. This is read, not driven. If the operator counts
   that dismissal as a refusal, it is T-179.1-87 at high and it blocks. See the first note.
2. **T-179.1-104 is worded for two of the three rows.** In the row where the cookies were cleared, the user who walked away
   does not stay signed in. The page only reads that way, with the draft still in storage. See the third note.
3. **"Nothing left on a shared computer" holds for the tab that signed out, and not for a second tab.** See the second note.

## Notes for the operator

- **Can anything other than the intended count reach the count's end? One thing can: a close the component is not told of.**
  The count starts only from a press of that button (`LogoutButton.svelte:89`), and the timer is the one thing that calls
  `leave` unasked (`LogoutButton.svelte:125`, `TimedModal.svelte:129`).

  | Route | Reaches it? | Why |
  |---|---|---|
  | A native close of the dialog | **Yes** | below |
  | Another tab | No | The frontend has no `storage` listener, `BroadcastChannel` or `onAuthStateChange`. `PersistedStateImpl` reads storage only in its constructor, so plan 44's "by another tab's storage" is not a live route to a guard either |
  | An open modal after a navigation inside the app | No | `afterNavigate` closes it and the close stops the timer (`LogoutButton.svelte:80-85`, `TimedModal.svelte:139-143`); an unmount clears it (`:101-103`). Unit `LogoutButton.svelte.test.ts:267,599`; E2E `spec.ts:344`, shown able to fail by `179.1-44-mut-count` |
  | A navigation that is refused | It stays, in plain sight | Back with an unsaved edit raises the native question. Cancel stops the navigation, so `afterNavigate` does not run, and the modal and its count go on. A timer that came due behind `window.confirm` fires as soon as it is answered |
  | A hidden tab's throttled timer | Only late | Throttling delays a `setTimeout` and never brings it forward, and `isOpen` is read when it fires |

  - **The native close.** `ModalContainer` learns of a close only through `handleClose`: Continue, the ✕, and a `keydown` of
    Escape on the document. Its `<dialog>` has no `cancel` or `close` handler (`ModalContainer.svelte:131`), where
    `DrawerHost.svelte:157` has one. The HTML Standard's close requests, which include Android's back button, close a modal
    dialog with no `keydown`. `isOpen` and `modalOpen` then stay true and the timer keeps running. The dialog is gone, and at
    the count's end `leave` runs, the user is signed out and the edit is discarded, with nothing on the screen meanwhile.
    A navigation still stops it.
  - **This is T-179.1-87's harm:** the session and the drafts destroyed for a user who refused. It also falls outside
    T-179.1-98's bound, "what the user was told".
  - **Why it is a warning and not an open threat.** Plan 44 declares Stay as Continue, the ✕ and Escape, and all three hold.
    ASVS 1 asks for a mitigation's presence and not for the absence of a bypass. And this is read, not driven: no run had
    an Android device, and the browser's behaviour comes from the standard. **No record accepts it, and the call is the
    operator's.** The likely fix is a `cancel` handler in `ModalContainer` that goes through `handleClose`. That reaches
    every modal, and no plan here lists the file.
  - **A limit in the other direction.** A timer that does not run signs nobody out: a suspended tab, a locked phone, a closed
    lid. A tab closed during the count ends it with the session and the draft kept. T-179.1-89's retirement says "a user who
    walks away no longer stays signed in" and does not name these, and neither does the UAT list. Read, not measured.
  - **The count can also discard what the modal never mentioned.** A draft restored on a page with no guard goes with
    `#reset`. With tasks left the modal speaks only of their counts, and with none a press removes the draft at once. This is
    the open entry *Log out on a page with no guard…*. Its text says it fits the UAT, but it is not on the list, and it still
    names the removed `confirmLeave`.

- **After a successful sign-out, what is left on a shared computer?** Nothing of the session in the tab that signed out, on
  the normal path. Less is cleared on the other three.

  | Path | Drafts in `localStorage` | Memory | Evidence |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Direct press, the modal's Log out, the count's end or a gate's Log out, with `goto` moving | Gone: `#reset` removes every key under the stem, for all users and entities (`contestantContext.svelte.ts:607`, `contestantUserDataState.svelte.ts:341-344`) | `#savedData`, `#staged`, `#draft` and `#entryFlow` cleared; a page's own edits unmount; `page.data.user`, `entities` and `contestantUserData` are replaced, as `goto` leaves `(protected)` with `invalidateAll` | E2E tests 3 and 7, one entity's key, shown able to fail by `179.1-44-mut-discard`; unit `contestantUserDataState.svelte.test.ts:665`. The full load has run only under `179.1-44-mut-dialog` |

  - **Left on every path, by design:** in `localStorage`, `appContext-userPreferences` (consent with its date, feedback and
    survey status) and `VoterContext-answerStore` (a voter's answers, if this browser used the voter app). In
    `sessionStorage`, `voterContext-firstQuestionId` and SvelteKit's scroll positions and empty snapshots, as no route exports
    `snapshot`. The Contestant App writes nothing to `sessionStorage`. None of these names an account, but the voter's
    answers are personal, and no sign-out touches them.
  - **What the tests do not see:** no test reads `sessionStorage`, memory, or a key other than that one entity's. No test
    opens two tabs.

- **T-179.1-103 and T-179.1-104: stated correctly? They reach the operator; each is stated incompletely.**
  - **T-179.1-104 reaches them everywhere plan 46 promised:** both first paragraphs, the first UAT item and a deferred entry.
    Its statement, "stays signed in", is right for rows 1 and 3 and **wrong for row 2**. There the cookies are cleared, as test
    10's `expectSignedOut` observes, so nobody is signed in. The page reads as signed in, the draft stays in storage, and by plan
    45's reading a save fails. After a reload the login screen has no Log out, so no `#reset` is reachable, and the draft stays until someone signs
    in. The summaries, the UAT item and the deferred title all say "signed in" without this.
  - **Three more limits of T-179.1-104.** It is filed as denial of service, while the harm the operator is asked about is
    the next person using the session, which is how T-179.1-103 is filed. "The message on the screen" does not hold for a
    `POST` that hangs: by reading, the count's end closes the modal, `leaving` stays up, the header's Log out silently does
    nothing, and no message comes until the browser gives up on the request. And test 9's draft line has never been seen red, as ledger row
    `45-7` says.
  - **T-179.1-103's facts hold, and I re-measured the part no run drove.** With an expired token during an outage, a press
    answers 502 with the cookies kept, a second press the same, and a press after recovery 200 with the cookies cleared, for
    both a refused connection and a 503. Three parts of the row are incomplete:
    - "The window needs an auth outage while the token is expired" leaves out the 90-second margin the earlier audit named
      (`EXPIRY_MARGIN_MS`).
    - "The user is told" holds only while that document stays. `#logoutFailed` is memory, so a reload during the outage lands on
      the earlier audit's false screen: login, no Log out, no message. `179.1-45-SUMMARY.md:135` says the user lands there, and
      not that nothing is said. A user who walked away is not told at all.
    - "As the operator's ruling says": the operator's only words on record are "let's add the error msg". The earlier audit had
      told them that telling the user does not clear these cookies, so they may have meant it. But the sentence that the cookies
      stay out of the fix comes from the orchestrator's source-coverage row, and no record shows them accepting the risk.
  - **T-179.1-103 reaches them by a table row, not by a question.** `179.1-46-SUMMARY.md:112` names it, with the risk. The UAT

    it, or overrule it", which T-179.1-104's does.
