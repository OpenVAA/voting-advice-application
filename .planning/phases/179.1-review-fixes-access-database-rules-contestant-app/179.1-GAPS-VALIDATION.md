## How to read this

- **COVERED:** a standing automated test asserts the truth. For a run-count truth, the run's `summary.json` shows it.
- **PARTIAL:** a test asserts part of the truth, and the rest is read, inferred or a one-off measurement.
- **MISSING:** no standing test asserts the truth. A one-off check may exist, and the row says so.
- **Seen red:** this column cites the ledger row and its outcome. "None" means the ledger has no row for it.
