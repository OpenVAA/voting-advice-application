## Rulings carried by this plan

1. **Three causes, confirmed.** The ✕ (`Modal.svelte`) and Escape and the native close (`ModalContainer.svelte`) close unconditionally, and `LogoutButton`'s `handleClose` hears of it after the fact. `TimedModal` calls `onTimeout` without awaiting it and then closes. `#runLogout` stands the guards down only after the sign-out answers, so `PreventNavigation` asks `window.confirm` for a link meanwhile. Measured in the diagnosis with the sign-out held 10 s: the dialog closed 0.6 s after Escape and after the ✕, and at the count's end; a link raised the native question in all three paths.
2. **`holdOpen`, opt-in.** `ModalContainer` gains `holdOpen?: boolean`, default `false`, which `Modal` passes on and `TimedModal` passes through. While it is up: the document Escape handler does nothing; the dialog's `cancel` event is prevented; a `close` the platform forces anyway is answered by opening the dialog again, with no callback. `closeModal()` always closes, so the navigation handler and the end of the sign-out close it as before. Every other consumer leaves it unset.
3. **Chromium's limit.** Measured in headless Chromium 153: a prevented `cancel` holds two Escapes in a row; the third has `cancelable` false and the dialog closes. Ruling 2's reopening covers that close. Safari, Firefox and Android Chrome are not measured; plan 66's list carries it.
4. **The ✕, with no visual change.** daisyUI 5 styles `.btn[aria-disabled=true]` (read in `node_modules/daisyui/components/button.css`), and G-10 has no visual grant. So the ✕ carries no new attribute; its click does nothing while held.
5. **The count waits.** At the count's end `TimedModal` awaits `onTimeout` and then closes if it is still open. `LogoutButton` passes `holdOpen={leaving}`, so the ✕ and Escape are held during the count's sign-out too, and its `leave` closes the modal when the sign-out settles, as now.
6. **Navigations wait.** `navigationGuards.ts` gains `holdNavigationWhile(run)`, which holds while `run` is pending, `isNavigationHeld()`, and `holdNavigation(navigation)`, which a `beforeNavigate` callback calls: while held, and not inside `leaveUnasked`, it cancels a navigation of any type but `leave` and remembers its destination. When `run` rejects, the remembered destination is followed with `goto`, where the page's guard asks as it does for any link; when it resolves, it is dropped. `LogoutButton` registers `beforeNavigate(holdNavigation)` and runs its `logout()` inside `holdNavigationWhile`. `PreventNavigation` asks nothing, and prevents no unload, while `isNavigationHeld()`: closing the tab during a sign-out is not stopped by a question, because the edit is lost either way.
7. **Where the hold lives.** In `LogoutButton`, whose `leaving` it follows, rather than in the context: the gates that call `logout` (identity provider, set-password, terms of use) open no modal and are not part of the ruling.
