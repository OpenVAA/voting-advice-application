5. **The announcement.** The page's `ErrorMessage` for these reasons carries `role="alert"` and an id. The page's `<h1>` takes `tabindex="-1"`, as the Contestant App's login heading has, so the focus reset (`focusNavigationTarget`, which focuses the first `<h1>`) lands on it, and carries `aria-describedby` naming the notice while one is shown. 6. **The browser test of the server's sign-out plants a run-out count.** No Admin App page writes a count: the Contestant App's logout modal does, and its sequence is driven in `contestant-entity-selection` (plans 63 to 75). Here a new fixture step writes `logout-pending` with a 32-hex id and no `logout-deadline` into the context of an admin signed in through the form in a session of its own, which is the state the browser is in after a count's deadline has passed with its tab closed; a link inside the Admin App whose page makes a request then meets the hook, the gate and the login page as a real one does.
