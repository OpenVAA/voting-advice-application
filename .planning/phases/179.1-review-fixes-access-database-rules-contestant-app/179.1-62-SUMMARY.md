# Phase 179.1 Plan 62: The logout modal and links wait for a running sign-out Summary

**An opt-in `holdOpen` on the modal stack keeps the logout modal open while its sign-out runs. The ✕, Escape and a forced close no longer close it. Its count now awaits `onTimeout`, and `holdNavigationWhile` and `holdNavigation` hold a link with no question, following it only after a failed sign-out.**

## For the operator's reading

What a user who is leaving now meets: after the modal's Log out, or at the end of its count, the modal stays on screen until the sign-out settles. Its ✕ and Escape do nothing, and the ✕ looks exactly as before. A close the browser forces, such as Chromium's third Escape in a row, opens the dialog again at once. A link pressed while any sign-out the Contestant App's Log out started is running asks nothing and waits. After a sign-out that worked, the user is on the login screen and the link is dropped. After one that failed, the link is followed, and the page's own guard asks its usual question if an edit is unsaved. No other modal of the application is held: the input defaults to off, and only the logout modal sets it.

What stays unproven:
- The Escape and back-gesture hold outside headless Chromium (ruling 3). Safari, Firefox and Android Chrome are not run. Whether the third Escape's forced close was reopened in the E2E run is inferred: the test sees the dialog open after the ✕ and three Escapes, and does not observe the close and reopen between them.
- Closing the tab during a sign-out asks nothing, by the ruling.
- The count test has nothing unsaved, so its empty `nativeDialogs` would also hold without the hold. A link over an unsaved edit during a sign-out, and a failed sign-out followed by the held link, are shown by unit cases only.
- A Back pressed during a sign-out is cancelled like a link. After a failure it is followed with `goto`, which goes forward to that address rather than back in the history. This is read from the code, not run.
- What VoiceOver, NVDA or TalkBack say of a ✕ that does nothing. The ruling gave it no attribute, because daisyUI styles `aria-disabled` and this gap has no visual grant.

Observed by the runs: the two E2E reds and greens, the unit reds and the mutation controls. Inferred: the forced-close reopen in Chromium's E2E run, and the Back path above.
