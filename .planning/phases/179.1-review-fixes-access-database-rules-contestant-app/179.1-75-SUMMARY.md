# Phase 179.1 Plan 75: The login screen says why after a count's end and a followed sign-out Summary

**The candidate context's `logout` now carries an optional reason to the login page as `errorMessage`: the logout modal's count's end passes `loggedOutAutomatically`, and a tab that follows a sign-out made in another tab goes to the login page with `loggedOutElsewhere`, so the Contestant App's login screen says why in both cases, through plan 74's notice.**

## For the operator's reading

A user who walked away from a tab where the logout modal was counting comes back to the login screen with the notice under the title: "You have been logged out automatically, because a logout you started was not cancelled in time." (in their locale). A user who presses Log out in one tab finds every other open tab of the Contestant App on the login screen at once, with the notice "You have been logged out in another tab or window." A Log out the user presses, from the header or in the modal, still ends on the login screen with no notice. Both sentences arrive on the address bar as `errorMessage` and reach the page through the notice and the heading's description that plan 74 built. No message key changed.

What stays unproven:

- **What a screen reader says.** The notice is asserted as the browser renders it. VoiceOver, NVDA and TalkBack go on the UAT list.
- **Every engine but Chromium**, and real phones.
- **The `fr` and `lb` sentences**, written by plan 74, are still flagged for a native reader.
