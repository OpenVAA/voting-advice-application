# Phase 179.1 Plan 11: the confirm page between a mailed link and its verification Summary

**A mailed link can now open `/auth/confirm`, a page that belongs to neither app.**
- **GET:** renders the link's fields in a form and spends nothing.
- **POST:** Continue posts the form, and the action verifies through `verifyMailedLink` (verify, claim, refresh, land, moved out of the callback).
- **Signed-in browser:** the page says that continuing signs the current account out, and Cancel keeps that account.

Nothing links to the page yet. Plan 12 hands the callback's links to it.

## Accomplishments

- **The route** `/auth/confirm` (`ROUTE.AuthConfirm`, beside `AuthCallback`). `appGateHandle` does not gate it: no `APP_GATES` row claims it, and `routeConsistency.test.ts` pins that.
  - **`load`:** reads `token_hash`, `type` and `next` and answers them, plus `signedIn` and, only when signed in, `cancelRoute`. It calls no auth method. A missing or empty token, or a type other than the three mailed ones, gets a 303 to the Contestant App's login with `authError`.
  - **`actions.default`:** reads the three fields from the posted form, not the address. When the request holds a session it calls `signOut({ scope: 'local' })`, then `verifyMailedLink`. SvelteKit's default `csrf.checkOrigin` guards it.
  - **No session leaves the server.** Only `signedIn` and the Cancel route do, and `assert:no-session-in-loads` passes.
- **The page** renders under the root layout as `+error.svelte` does, with `MainContent` inside `<main>`:
  - the title and the intro sentence for the link's type;
  - for a signed-in browser, the notice (`auth-confirm-signed-in`);
  - the hidden-field form posted with `use:enhance`; a second press while a post runs is cancelled;
  - Continue (`auth-confirm-continue`) and, when signed in, a Cancel link (`auth-confirm-cancel`).
  - Its root carries `data-testid="auth-confirm-page"` and `data-type`. Nothing is destructured from a context.
- **The module** `verifyMailedLink({ tokenHash, type, next, locals, fetch })`:
  - It holds `REFUSED_TYPES`, `ADDRESS_TAKEN_FAILURE`, `emailChangeFailureRoute`, `isAddressTakenFailure` and `landingRoute`, moved with their TSDoc, and throws the same 303s.
  - It also exports `MAILED_LINK_TYPES` and `isMailedLinkType`.
  - The callback's GET only reads the params and calls the module. Its `server.test.ts` passes unchanged.
- **Behaviour change (PL-09):** a failed `email_change` confirmed from a signed-in browser now lands on the login page, because Continue signs out first. A unit case pins it.
- **The strings:** `authConfirm.json` in seven locales, the inlang path, and `translationKey.ts` regenerated. The namespace guard exits 0, with 749 keys.
- **The tests:**
  - three `invitation-landing` cases;
  - four scans: `auth-confirm-signed-out` in `contestant-entry-scan` and `auth-confirm-signed-in` in `contestant-a11y-scan`, each in light and dark;
  - unit cases in five frontend test files and in `tests/tests/utils/mailedLink.test.ts`.
