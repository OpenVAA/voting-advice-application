# Phase 179.1 Plan 18: the Check your email view as the operator read it Summary

**The Check your email view now names the typed address in bold in its first sentence: "We’ve sent an email to <strong>address</strong>. Click on the link in it to sign in." The address is escaped by the new `escapeHtml` before it goes into the sentence, and the sentence goes through `sanitizeHtml`. The Sent to list is gone. Under the hints comes "If you have problems receiving the email, contact support.", where the words "contact support" are the link. Send again follows inside the panel. Its wait is in the button itself, "Send again (in 12s)", as a new `Button` suffix in normal weight and the secondary colour. The entity home's reminder, the settings page and CI1 show the same button.**
