## Rulings carried by this plan

1. **The confirm action (A2).** In `routes/auth/confirm/+page.server.ts`, the default action:
   - reads and refuses the form's fields as now; a refused post withdraws nothing, as now;
   - no longer withdraws the count itself;
   - when the request holds a session, by `readHeldSession`, signs it out with scope `local`, reading both failure forms as `signOutToLogin` does: an `{ error }` answer and a rejection;
   - on a failure, logs the error's name, then asks `isSessionGone(locals.supabase)` from `$lib/api/adapters/supabase/sessionGone`. A session left, or no answer, throws `error(503, { message: t('error.default') })`: the failure is shown, nothing is verified and the count is kept. A failure that left no session goes on;
   - then hands the fields, `locals`, `cookies`, `fetch`, `request` and `url` to `verifyMailedLink`.

   Where the withdrawal sits is the review's alternative: right after a `verifyOtp` that answered a session. That meets the ruling's condition, because verification is reached only with no session held or after the held sign-out worked or found the session gone. It also closes the audit's outage reading on this page, where `readHeldSession` takes a session that cannot be read for none. The ruling's own sketch withdraws before verification in that branch, which would leave that reading open. The 503 is the form `signOutToLogin` takes for the same state, with copy that exists (rule 4).

   The module docblock's paragraph on the action, and its sentence beginning `Because the sign-out comes first`, are restated to match. The sentence on a session that cannot be read stays. The adapter-boundary allowlist's comment on this file stays true (rule 8).
2. **`verifyMailedLink`.**
   - Its options gain `request` and `url`, and `cookies` widens to the request's whole `Cookies`, the type `withdrawLogoutCount` takes. The three are passed to it as `{ cookies, request, url }`.
   - Right after `verifyOtp`, when it answered no error and a session, it calls `withdrawLogoutCount({ cookies, request, url })`. That comes before the `email_change` failure check, the arrival marker and the claim.
   - Every other path leaves both cookies: a missing or refused type, a failed verification, an `email_change` that answered no session.
   - Its TSDoc says so, and that the confirm page is its one caller.
3. **The gated sign-in actions: the diagnosis first.** `$U/179.1-89-diagnosis.txt` holds the lines read at HEAD and the conclusion:
   - `hooks.server.ts` `appGateHandle` redirects an entry route only when `safeGetSession` answers a session;
   - `safeGetSession.ts` answers no session when `getUser()` errors;
   - each of the three actions calls `withdrawLogoutCount(event)` before its helper;
   - the helpers' sign-in returns its error without touching a held session (`passwordLogin.ts` `signIn`, `invitationCodeLogin.ts`).
   The reading holds when every line says so, and when Tasks 2 and 3's refusal cases are red at HEAD. A reading that does not hold stops the plan with the file, and the planner revises Tasks 2 and 3.
4. **The gated sign-in actions: the fix.** In `admin/login`, `contestant/login/email` and `contestant/register/sign-in`, the one `withdrawLogoutCount(event)` moves to after the line that returns the helper's refusal, and before the redirect. So it runs only on an `ok` outcome, in the response that sets the session's cookies. On success that response is the same as today, since a response's cookies are written when it is sent. Every refusal now keeps the count: wrong credentials, a refused grant, no session, a refused registration guard. Each action's docblock sentence on the withdrawal is restated, and `register/sign-in` keeps its guard first.
5. **`logoutCount.ts`.** Its docblock's paragraph on the callers is made true of every call site, listed with `git grep -n -E "^[[:space:]]+withdrawLogoutCount\(" -- apps/frontend/src`:
   - the three gated sign-in actions, once their helper admitted the session;
   - `verifyMailedLink`, for the confirm page, and the identity provider's callback, once `verifyOtp` has set it;
   - the Contestant App's protected loads (`signOutToLogin`), once their sign-out has worked or found the session gone;
   - the logout endpoint, before its sign-out, deliberately.
   Its first sentence's clause on every sign-in and every sign-out the server makes is made true too. Comment lines alone change.
6. **The auth guide (C9).** In `backend/authentication/+page.md`, the bullet beginning `A browser that never makes another request keeps the session`, two sentences are restated from what the code now does, and every other sentence stays word for word:
   - the one beginning `Every sign-in the application makes`: the sign-ins expire both cookies through `withdrawLogoutCount` once they have signed the user in. That is the password and code sign-ins once their helper has admitted the session, and the confirm page and the identity provider's callback once `verifyOtp` has set it. A refused or failed sign-in keeps the count;
   - the one beginning `A sign-out the server makes on its own`: such a sign-out, other than the hook's, expires them only once it has worked or found the session gone. The confirm page's, before a verification, leaves them to that verification. A failed one with a session left keeps the count for the next request, as the hook's own does, and the confirm page then shows the error page.
   The words `found the session gone`, `admitted the session` and `confirm page` appear in the bullet.
