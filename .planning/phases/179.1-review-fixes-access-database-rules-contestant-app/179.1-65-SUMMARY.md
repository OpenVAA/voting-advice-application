# Phase 179.1 Plan 65: Controls at the body's text size, app-wide Summary

**Buttons, inputs, selects and textareas are back at the body's 15 px across the app. The cause was a size rule in `@layer components` that daisyUI 5's sublayered rules beat. That rule and the iOS 16 px floor now sit as un-sublayered `:where()` rules in `@layer utilities`, where they win. A text utility named on an element still beats them. A new browser test was red at 12 px and is green now, and the two voter-results visual baselines were regenerated in the pinned container.**

## For the operator's reading

**What a user now sees.**
- What a user types into the sign-in screen's email and password fields is at 15 px, the body's size. It was 12 px.
- The sign-in button and every other button is at 15 px. Plain buttons were 14 px; small buttons such as a modal's close button were 12 px.
- The same holds for every `.input`, `.select` and `.textarea` in the app, the shared `Input` and `Select` included.
- Labels are unchanged.
- An element that names its own text size (`text-sm`, `text-xs`, `text-lg`) keeps it.
- On the results page the visible change is the toolbar's buttons (the filter button and the sort and search controls). This is the whole of the pixel difference in the two regenerated baselines.

**The blast radius (ruling 3).** Measured by the scratch probe `tests/e2e-runs/179.1-unit/179.1-65-blast-radius-probe.mjs` on the visual recipe's server. Before = the same page with the size rule's two CSSOM entries deleted; after = as served. Chromium and WebKit agree on every row (390x844):

No text utility was overridden, so ruling 3's stop condition did not apply. The sized variants (`.btn-sm`, `.input-sm`, `.select-sm`) now take 15 px too, which is how the app read before the Tailwind 4 bump. Their height and padding are daisyUI's own and did not change.

**What stays unproven** (plan 66's UAT list):
- On iOS Safari, that form controls compute 16 px and that focusing one does not zoom the page. Neither engine here supports `-webkit-touch-callout`, so no run took the floor's branch.
- The look at phone widths (320 to 390 px) and in dark mode. The baselines cover two pages, at 1280 and 390, in light only.
- Whether 15 px in a `.btn-sm` (a modal's ✕, the Jobs feature's small buttons) reads well in its row.

Observed:
