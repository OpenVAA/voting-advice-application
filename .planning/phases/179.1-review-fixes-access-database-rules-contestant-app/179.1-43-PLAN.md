## Rulings carried by this plan

1. **Why `common.json` for both.** `common.logoutFailed` is read by both apps' Log out, so it belongs there. `common.logoutUnsavedChanges` would sit more naturally in `contestantApp.logoutModal.json`, but two catalogue files in seven locales are fourteen files plus the type, over the plan's line. Beside `common.logout` it is still easy to find. No key is renamed or moved.

2. **The modal's title takes no new key.** With only unsaved edits, the modal's existing title, `contestantApp.logoutModal.title` ("Some of Your Data Is Still Missing"), would be false, so plan 44 uses `common.logout` there. Its Stay label stays `contestantApp.logoutModal.continue`. Its count stays `contestantApp.logoutModal.ingress`.

3. **The failure's wording is decided by the three rows of `179.1-CR01-SECURITY.md`'s table.** The sentence must be true when the session is alive (first row), when this browser's cookies were cleared despite the 502 (second row), and when the cookies are kept and the service is down (third row). So it says the logout could not be confirmed and that the user may still be logged in, and it claims neither state. It says nothing of unsaved changes, because a direct press has none. Plan 45 reads the rows against the code.

4. **The texts are agent-written.** No native speaker has read them. Estonian uses the informal "you", French uses the catalogue's inclusive `·e` and typographic apostrophe, and English says "log out" and "logged in", as the operator's edits of 2026-11-01 do (D-09).

5. **Nothing else moves.** No other string is edited. No screen reads these keys in this plan. The new-copy UAT item is plan 46's. Nothing under `apps/supabase` or `packages/supabase-types`.

6. **Detectors and probes.** `assumption-delta` and `api-coverage` detected nothing. The schema gate does not apply. The spec-less probe fallback is skipped, because the phase has no requirement id to probe beyond E2E-03.
