<objective>
Sweep the frontend's context layer, dynamic components and Supabase adapter surface — the part of the tree
whose comments most often *are* the invariant.

**Scale note, so a later reader does not think this drifted.** CONTEXT.md fact 7 puts `apps` at 219
planning-reference comment lines. `152-RESEARCH.md` § 4 re-measured at HEAD `22c2542e3` and found **290** —
the largest undercount of the three trees, at +33% — inside a repo-wide **888 citation lines / 942 union /
946 occurrences / 642 spans across 278 files** against CONTEXT.md's 817. The measured figures govern per
CONTEXT.md § 0.1. Roughly 90 of the apps spans belong to this plan; the register's `152-10` partition list is
authoritative.

**Why this partition needs the most careful judgement in the phase.** CLAUDE.md devotes its longest section to
the Svelte 5 Context Destructuring Rule, and calls the defect it guards *"the single most-repeated defect in
this codebase's history."* The comments in `apps/frontend/src/lib/contexts/**` are where that rule is restated
at the call sites it governs — including the later carve-out for the identity-stable accessor whose reference
never changes and whose only reactive signal is a private version counter, which must be read directly inside
the consuming tracking scope rather than bound to an intermediate alias. Those comments are **live
invariants**, not narrative. D-A1's rewrite-not-delete default applies with full force: strip the citation,
keep every word of the rule.

**`152-CONTEXT.md` `<open>` item 3, disposed of here.** The PR #869 comment at the Supabase data provider's
base-object construction has two halves: *"Aren't the fields already typed by `toDataObject`? They should be"*
— a **typing** question — and *"remove line breaks from comment"*. `152-RESEARCH.md` § 10.2 confirms the split.
**The typing half belongs to Phase 157 (Adapter Boundary & Typing)**, whose goal is stated in the roadmap as
*"Data crossing the Supabase boundary is validated into its type rather than cast into it"* — the comment
asserts the fields are explicitly typed while the lines below it are a wall of casts, which is exactly what
Phase 157 exists to resolve. **The line-break half belongs to Phase 152, but not to this plan**: that comment
carries no planning reference, so it is not in this plan's class at all; it is in the D-A4 class and only
there, and plan `152-14` owns it under an operator decision. This plan's obligation is to **record the
Phase-157 half in its SUMMARY in a findable form**, and to leave that comment's sentence semantically
unchanged so Phase 157 can rewrite it wholesale. Recorded here so it is not lost between the two phases.

Purpose: leave the reactive layer and the adapter explained by their comments, with every destructure-trap
invariant and every measured timing hazard intact.
Output: a swept context, component and adapter surface, proven comment-only.
</objective>
