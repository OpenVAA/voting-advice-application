<objective>
Sweep the planning narrative out of the remaining `packages/**` workspaces — the core data, matching, filters,
shared-app, shared-config and type-generation packages that everything else in the monorepo depends on.

**Scale note, so a later reader does not think this drifted.** CONTEXT.md fact 7 puts `packages` at 336
planning-reference comment lines. `152-RESEARCH.md` § 4 re-measured at HEAD `22c2542e3` and found **341** in
packages, inside a repo-wide **888 citation lines / 942 union / 946 occurrences / 642 spans across 278 files**
against CONTEXT.md's 817. The measured figures govern per CONTEXT.md § 0.1. Roughly 128 of the packages spans
sit outside dev-seed and belong to this plan; the register's `152-09` partition list is authoritative.

**These packages carry the highest ratio of load-bearing comments in the repo.** The data model's smart-default
and missing-value conventions, the matching algorithm's subdimension handling for categorical questions, the
filter rule semantics, and the shared ESLint configuration's ordering groups are all documented in comments
that explain non-obvious code. D-A1's default is exactly right here and its rejection of option (d) —
delete-by-default *"destroys measured hazard notes that no other document holds"* — bites hardest in this tree.

**One hard prohibition, measured.** `packages/shared-config/eslint.config.mjs` carries five unicode-escape
occurrences inside **string literals** used as import-sort regexes. They are not comments. The escape rule in
`scripts/assert-comment-hygiene.mjs` is comment-scoped precisely so it cannot reach them, and an over-eager
hand edit here would break lint repo-wide. Do not touch them, and do not "harmonise" them with the docstring
fix that plan `152-01` made in a Svelte component.

Purpose: leave the shared packages explained by their comments, with every convention and hazard note intact
and every configuration byte unchanged.
Output: a swept packages surface, proven comment-only.
</objective>
