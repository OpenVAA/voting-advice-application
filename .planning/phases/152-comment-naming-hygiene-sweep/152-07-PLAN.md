<objective>
Sweep the planning narrative out of the E2E specs — the heaviest planning-narrative carriers in the repository,
and, as D-A1's rejection of option (c) puts it, *"read most often by agents."*

That rejection is why this work exists at all. Skipping the tests tree was proposed and refused in terms:
*"the E2E specs are the heaviest planning-narrative carriers in the repo and are read most often by agents."*

**Scale note, so a later reader does not think this drifted.** CONTEXT.md fact 7 puts the tests tree at 223
planning-reference comment lines. `152-RESEARCH.md` § 4 re-measured at HEAD `22c2542e3` and found **257** in
tests (888 repo-wide, 942 including narrative-only lines, 946 occurrences, 642 spans across 278 files). The
measured figure governs per CONTEXT.md § 0.1. The single heaviest spec measured 13 reference spans.

**The judgement standard is D-A1's.** Strip the citation; if what remains states something live about the code
in front of it, rewrite it to say that without the reference; only pure narrative about a past edit is
deleted. Option (d) — delete by default — was rejected because it *"destroys measured hazard notes that no
other document holds."* In an E2E spec those notes are frequently the only in-tree record of a diagnosed race,
and under CLAUDE.md's cardinal rule a reintroduced flake is a phase-blocking failure, not an inconvenience.

**Titles are out of scope here, deliberately.** Review comment #4 asks for planning references to be removed
from test *names*, and criterion 5 simultaneously treats a test title as a legitimately-declined match. Both
are satisfied by splitting them: the codemod and this plan decline every title, and plan `152-13` renames them
deliberately with a cross-check against the E2E run registers and a full-suite gate. The disposition is
recorded in `152-RESIDUE-REGISTER.md`; this plan simply must not pre-empt it.

Purpose: leave the specs readable as tests rather than as a changelog, without reopening a single diagnosed
flake.
Output: a swept spec tree, proven comment-only.
</objective>
