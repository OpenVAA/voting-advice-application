<objective>
Criterion 13: the two subtree server loads stop serialising a whole session — and therefore a
refresh token — into the hydration payload of every authenticated page, and a repository-level
assertion makes a fourth load unable to reintroduce the class quietly.

Purpose: the phase-157.2 review's CR-02 fix removed this from the ROOT load, where nothing read
it, and wrote the projection rule into that file's own docstring. These two are read — by the
authentication context's derived flag and by the identity helper's ancestor pre-check — so
narrowing them is a behavioural change this phase owns. Without the durable assertion a fourth
load reintroduces the class, which is the roadmap's own stated reason for requiring one.

Output: two narrowed loads, a narrowed structural type, a chained assertion script with a real
corpus and a red fixture, and the negative-control rows that prove the guard guards.
</objective>
