<objective>
Resolve criterion 1's own named exemplar. `PasswordSetter.svelte:55` and `:58` are pure functions of
their inputs and therefore pass criterion 1's semantic test, but both write to props declared with
two-way binding, which Svelte 5 forbids a derived value from holding. That collision is a decision, not
an executor call, so this plan locks the current behaviour with a test, puts the three costed options in
front of the operator, and implements the answer.

Purpose: D-H1 chose "census all, convert only the unambiguous ones" precisely to bound the blast radius
of contract changes. This plan makes the boundary explicit at the one site the reviewer pointed at, and
produces the evidence rows the census needs for the three sites the plan-time hand review demoted.

Output: a locked-in unit test for the validity and error outputs, an operator decision on record, the
implemented disposition, and the recorded evidence for the three demoted sites.
</objective>
