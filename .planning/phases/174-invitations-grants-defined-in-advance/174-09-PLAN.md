## Rulings and deviations carried by this plan

1. **OQ-3.** `toCallbackUrl` stays for the journey's password-reset step; its two registration uses go. The
   closing search for it is handed to Phase 178, and this plan asserts that the step is its only caller.
2. **OQ-4.** The mailed link is opened through `mailedLink`: shape asserted, origin replaced, nothing else.
3. **Ruling 16 of the phase.** The callback's `invite` arm is removed and that type is refused before
   verification.
4. **Both entity types.** The browser path is shown for a candidate (the journey); an organization's
   invitation is shown at the API level by the contract spec of 174-06 and in pgTAP. No browser test opens
   the dedicated organization, which has no nomination.
5. **Ruling 18 of the phase.** The landing spec shows what happens to a project-scope promise: a returning
   user reaches the "nothing to edit" view through the callback, and a project admin reaches the Admin App
   through its login. A first-time invitee with no contestant grant, who is refused by the login form after
   setting a password, is recorded for Phase 178 and not asserted as desirable.
6. **The registration subject is matched exactly**, not by the loose pattern the old mail needed.
