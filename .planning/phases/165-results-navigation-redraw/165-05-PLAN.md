<objective>
Make the extended-question-info drawer a second consumer of the same app-wide host, exercise it end to
end, and retire the two per-route drawer components that are left with no callers.

Purpose: criterion 5 names both overlays in terms - the entity-details overlay AND the
extended-question-info drawer - and spike 034's verdict was `PARTIAL` precisely because the
question-info path was never exercised. D-11 carries that as a standing obligation and D-13 is what
discharges it.

D-13 was **amended on 2026-09-23** after research measured the original landing site red, and the
amendment is an operator ruling. The work lands on the permission-scoped interactive-info template, not
on the base E2E dataset. Flipping the base dataset's interactive-info flag was measured to make the
voter journey's hard visibility assertion on the inline expander fail - a cardinal failure under the
project's E2E rule, measured rather than predicted. The permission-scoped template already ships the
flag enabled over purpose-built info questions, already has its own project chain, and its spec already
opens the popup. So D-13's words are satisfied by a template that exists, and the work is the migration
plus explicit open-and-close-through-the-host assertions.

Consequence the plan carries forward: the "widest blast radius in the phase" warning attached to D-13 is
**withdrawn**. This plan changes no seed dataset and cannot move a visual baseline. Its remaining hazard
is narrower and sharper - the popup testid and what the existing fixture asserts with it.

Output: the question-info button opening through the host, the fixture and spec updated to match, and
the two orphaned drawer components deleted with their types and barrel entries.
</objective>
