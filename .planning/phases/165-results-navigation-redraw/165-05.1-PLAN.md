<objective>
Close the regression Phase 165 introduced: `voter-journey` EQTYP-02 fails intermittently at a measured
37.5% (6/16 project-scoped runs) on the phase branch and 0% (0/22) on `integration/ship-12-squash`,
Fisher's exact p = 0.0029.

The defect is latent base code. `AccordionSelect.svelte` initialises `expanded` once from `activeIndex`
and never reconciles it. On the base branch every results navigation reran the `(located)` load and
blanked the subtree, so the component remounted and that stale state was reset by accident. Criterion 1
removes that teardown on purpose — the subtree now persists — and the stale `expanded` survives with it.

Fix the component, not the symptom. Do NOT reintroduce a remount, and do NOT weaken the untracked URL
reads in `(located)/+layout.ts`: that persistence is criterion 1 and the phase exists to deliver it.

Full evidence, both symptom signatures, the time-ordering control and the classifier trap are in
`165-BASE-FLAKE-MEASUREMENT.md`.
</objective>
