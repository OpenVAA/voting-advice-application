## Deviations from CONTEXT.md for operator review

1. **D-13 offered two ways to keep the journey working (the registration helper confirms the row, or counts
   are adjusted). Neither is used.** Research measured that no spec asserts the unregistered candidate's
   voter-side visibility after it registers, and that the journey reads its own row through its editor grant,
   independent of `confirmed`. So that candidate stays unconfirmed and never becomes public during the run.
   One assumption is verified only by this plan's E2E run: that the journey's preview step still shows the
   portrait of an unconfirmed candidate. If it does not, the remedy is D-13's other branch (the harness
   confirms that one row when it registers), applied in Task 3 and reported.
2. **The conditions and the column are removed in two schema commits** (this plan, then 172-07), with a full
   E2E run after each. D-20 lists both under one contract plan.
3. **The companions of the stripped clause in `05`, `18` and `25`** (the assertions that pin the clause to
   exactly the two candidate policies) **are deleted, not inverted.** D-15 says deleted cases are not rewritten
   as positive mirrors; the inverse census added in 172-07 covers "no policy names it".
4. **Two count-based pgTAP assertions that hold no terms-of-use string are known dependents of the column**
   (`09`: the number of columns in the candidates self-edit grant; `21`: the number of output columns of the
   user-data RPC). They change in 172-07, with the column. Any further count-based dependent this plan's
   pgTAP run surfaces is fixed at its cause and reported.
5. **Confirmed means public, registered or not** (D-13, restated because it is the security-relevant
   consequence): a custom seed template that produces confirmed, nominated candidates without the column
   makes them visible to voters after this plan, where they were hidden before.

## File count: why these twenty files stay in this plan

This plan lists twenty files, above the per-plan file threshold. The list was reviewed for anything that an
ordering constraint does not force here; nothing can leave without breaking one.

- **Fourteen files are one change and land together** (Tasks 1 and 2). The four schema files and the
  regenerated migration: the condition leaves all four sites at once, or a candidate's row, its nomination
  and its files would disagree about visibility. The six pgTAP files that assert the condition, which fail
  the moment it is gone, and the phase's own pgTAP file, whose trio is this change's proof. The two files of
  the control rows (`e2e/base.ts`, `base.test.ts`): without `confirmed: false` those two rows become public
  the moment the condition is gone.
- **Six files are the template edits of Task 3, and they have a window, not a free position.** They cannot
  move earlier than this plan's schema commit: while the condition exists, a seeded candidate without the
  column is invisible to voters, so the plan boundary before that commit would be E2E-red. They cannot move
  later than the commit of 172-07 that drops the column: a template row stating a column that does not exist
  fails the import and the dev-seed typecheck. The only other position inside the window is the start of
  172-07, which is already the larger plan because the column-drop commit is atomic. A tenth plan between
  the two would hold these six files and nothing else.
- Two of those six are comment-only (`packages/dev-seed/src/supabaseAdminClient.ts` and the integration
  test). They state a rule this plan's schema change makes false, so they are corrected with it.
