<objective>
Turn the extension into evidence: the two catch halves that give the zero its meaning, and the suite gates
that say the phase can ship.

Purpose: the candidate surfaces were measured at zero across 42 scans before a byte moved. That makes a
green candidate gate the least informative possible result — it is exactly what a gate pointed at nothing
would also produce. The only observation that separates those two worlds is a defect the old tree missed
and the new one names, run with the same instrument on both sides. `147-01` took the missing halves; this
plan takes the naming halves and pairs them.

The raw-key half carries a second, sharper claim. Criterion 3 does not merely ask that the new scan catch
the injection; it asks that the two named matchers still pass while it does. Both verdicts belong to one
run, because it is their simultaneity that shows why patching the matchers was never the fix.

Output: six filled rows, an empty unfilled-cell count, and a suite that is green three times over with the
candidate scans blocking.
</objective>
