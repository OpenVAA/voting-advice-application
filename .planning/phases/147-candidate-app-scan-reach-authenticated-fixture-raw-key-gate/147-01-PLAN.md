Take the two blindness halves on a tree that still carries the old configuration, and open the register
that the rest of the phase fills.

Purpose: `147-SCOUT-INVENTORY.md` measured the candidate `(protected)` surfaces at **0 violations across
42 scans**. That zero is the phase's foundation and it is also its trap: a gate wired onto an
already-clean surface turns green whether or not it is looking at anything. The only thing that can tell

the raw-key half: ROADMAP criterion 3 asks for the injection to PASS at the two named sites, and "passes"
is only meaningful once "the assertion actually ran and was green un-injected" is on the record beside it.

helper the rest of the phase uses, and a tree byte-identical to the one this plan started on.
