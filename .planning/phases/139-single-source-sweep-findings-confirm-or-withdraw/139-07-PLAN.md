<objective>
Propagate the outcome, and prove the tree was left exactly as found.

Criterion 4 says a withdrawn finding must be struck from the audit with its reasoning and ASSERT-07's
scope edited down "in this ROADMAP and in `REQUIREMENTS.md`" — and that the shrink must be visible in
the record, not silent. Research flagged as an open assumption whether the ROADMAP's Phase 142 block
actually enumerates the findings. It does. Phase 142's criterion 2 names F15 and its three sub-sites
inline in the criterion prose, and criterion 3 names F16, F17, F18 and "each of the six F20 sites". So
there are **three** propagation targets, not two, and the third is an in-sentence edit rather than a
struck line. A withdrawal that touched only `REQUIREMENTS.md` would leave Phase 142's success criteria
demanding remediation of a finding this phase struck.

The zero case matters as much as the non-zero one. Research predicts PASS for every correctly-designed
injection, which under D-01 and D-02 points at a set of confirmations and few or no withdrawals. "No
withdrawals" must be recorded as a positive statement with all three targets named and their current
enumerations quoted, not expressed as the absence of an edit — otherwise a later reader cannot tell
whether the propagation was checked and found unnecessary, or simply forgotten.

Finally, the phase's other deliverable is a non-deliverable: a source tree byte-identical to the one
it started with. Task 3 proves it across all seven vehicles rather than trusting the per-task gates.

**This plan ships zero product code and performs no injection.** It edits four planning documents.

Purpose: ASSERT-01 — and the criterion-4 half of the phase, which is what makes a withdrawal shrink
scope rather than surprise Phase 142 mid-remediation.

Output: `139-VERDICTS.md` §§ 6 and 9; conditional edits to the audit, `REQUIREMENTS.md` and
`ROADMAP.md`; the restored-corpus evidence.
</objective>
