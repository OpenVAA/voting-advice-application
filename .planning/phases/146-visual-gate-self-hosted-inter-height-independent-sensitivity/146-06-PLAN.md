<objective>
Settle, by measurement, the one question research deliberately left open — **does the variable-to-static
switch move pixels, and by how much?** — and prove that a broken same-origin font path now fails by
name.

Purpose: D-09 A argued the vendored faces are rasterisation-neutral, and that argument is correct about
*which weights render* and silent about *how they are delivered*. Google serves a single **variable**
woff2 for the two-weight request; the vendored files are the corresponding **static** instances,
byte-identical to Google's own single-weight statics. Whether FreeType's variable-font instancing and
static rasterisation of the same design agree at these sizes is unmeasured, and research was explicit
that it is **not to be assumed either way**.

This is the task research names as *"the task most likely to be omitted"* — it exists because research
disproved a premise, not because a decision asked for it — and it has exactly one window. The delta can
only be measured against the old baselines; `146-07` rewrites them.

The second half closes the other measured blindness. `settleFonts`' docblock claims it converts a
font-load failure into a named assertion. It does not, and never did: with zero `@font-face` rules
present, `document.fonts.check('1em Inter')` returns `true`. That matters far beyond tidiness here — a
wrong vendored path during the re-baseline would record fallback-font pixels as the new committed
truth, silently and permanently.

Output: four recorded diff counts regardless of size, four verdicts at the shipped cap, and a
two-directional proof that the guard catches what `settleFonts` cannot.
</objective>

<polarity>
## ⚠ In `146-06`, there is no wrong answer to the delta — only an unrecorded one

- **A non-zero delta is fine.** It means the delivery switch moved pixels, `146-07`'s re-baseline
  absorbs it deliberately, and the number is in the record so the next person does not have to
  rediscover it. Do not treat it as a defect.
- **A zero delta is fine.** It means D-09 A's neutrality claim held under measurement, which is
  information worth having precisely because it was an open question.
- **An unrecorded delta is the failure.** A baseline that passes at zero tolerance contributes the
  number `0`, not a blank.
- `F2-BOGUS-RED` is expected to **FAIL**, and to fail **by name**. A whole-page screenshot diff instead
  of a named guard failure would mean the N-2 hole is still open and the guard needs fixing before
  `146-07` runs `--update-snapshots=all` against it.
- `settleFonts` **passing** during the bogus half is the expected result, not an anomaly. It is N-2's
  truth table, re-observed.
</polarity>
