<assumption_delta_decision>
## The genuine delta this plan lands — recorded, not re-opened

The assumption-delta detector fired (`kind: chosen`) on *"The chosen mechanism — absolute
`maxDiffPixels` cap vs bounded/element-scoped capture"*. The delta is real and it is **already decided**
by the locked decisions. It is recorded here because this is the plan that changes the config, and a
future reader of `tests/playwright.config.ts` needs the framing.

- **Primary noun:** the **absolute per-baseline pixel budget** (`maxDiffPixels`).
- **Decision: `promote`.** `maxDiffPixelRatio` was *the* budget. It is **demoted** to a
  **small-baseline floor** (D-03 A). This is a promotion, not an add-alongside: the cap becomes the
  operative budget on all four baselines the suite has today, and the ratio binds only where
  `0.01 · area < cap`.
- **Why it is not add-alongside debt:** `Math.min` (verified at `comparators.js:88-96`) makes the
  combination **monotonically strictness-increasing**, so keeping both knobs cannot loosen any
  baseline. What the config comment must do is state **which one is primary** — the existing comment
  at `tests/playwright.config.ts:314` reads as if the ratio *is* the budget, and that reading becomes
  wrong the moment this plan lands.
- **Companion invariant — accepted, because it is already being built:** the D-06 height-independence
  control **is** the invariant test for this generalisation. It goes red the instant the budget becomes
  height-coupled again. Do not add a second one.
</assumption_delta_decision>

<objective>
Install the cap — the first product byte of this phase — and prove it does the two things it was chosen
to do: **catch** the regression the old configuration missed, and **stay flat** as a page grows taller.

Purpose: `maxDiffPixelRatio: 0.01` gives `voter-results-desktop` (1280×3684) a 47,155 px tolerance and
`candidate-preview-mobile` (390×924) a 3,603 px tolerance — one knob, a thirteen-fold spread, and the
tolerance rises automatically every time a page gets longer. That is why ~19,484 px of visible damage
sat at 0.41 % of budget and passed. An absolute cap combined by `Math.min` removes the coupling without
removing the ratio: the ratio survives as a floor for the small baselines, where it is the stricter of
the two.

Ordering note: this plan and `146-05` (the font) are **not** safely parallel and are not to be
re-arranged into parallel waves. Both feed the same re-baseline, and `146-06`'s font-delta measurement
must be taken against the cap already in force — otherwise *"did the pixels move?"* and *"did the
verdict move?"* are confounded and neither number means anything.

Output: one changed config block with its derivation in the comment, the catch half of the negative
control, and a height-independence control whose artefact is the ledger rather than a fifth PNG.
</objective>
