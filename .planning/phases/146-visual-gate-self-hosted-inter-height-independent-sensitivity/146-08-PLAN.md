<objective>
Turn everything the phase built into observed gate results: the egress-blocked green run VGATE-04
names, the consecutive-run proof VGATE-06 names, and the two structural checks that stop a green from
meaning nothing.

Purpose: this milestone's standing rule is that a check which has not been observed failing against a
realistic injected regression is not satisfied, however green the suite looks. The corollary is that a
green must also be observed under the conditions the requirement actually specifies — with the runner's
egress blocked, across consecutive runs, at CI's own invocation, and with the gate's own integrity
checks provably untouched.

Criterion 5 asks for at least three consecutive runs. D-17 takes **five** at a stricter posture than CI
plus **one** at CI's literal invocation, because the run-4 anomaly appeared once in five runs and three
has poor power to re-observe a roughly one-in-five event. Those six runs also give that anomaly a
second, independent chance to appear after `146-07`'s three bounded attempts.

Output: nine filled register rows, a complete ledger with its corpus asserted about itself, and a
phase whose gates are green at one HEAD.
</objective>
