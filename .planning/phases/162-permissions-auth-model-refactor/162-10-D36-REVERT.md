## 2. What changed, and what deliberately did not

**Changed — the composition, and only the composition.**

1. `public.entity_is_anon_visible (entity_type, uuid)` is **deleted** from `301-auth-functions.sql`. It
   is regenerated out of the single migration; no numbered migration was added and
   `00001_initial_schema.sql` was not hand-edited.
2. The four `TO anon` entity SELECT policies assemble the rule in their own qual.
3. The four `TO authenticated` entity SELECT policies assemble the same rule as their **fourth
   disjunct**, after the three `user_can` disjuncts, which are untouched.
4. `public.nomination_entities_confirmed` is restored to its pre-162-10 four-arm body.
5. `packages/supabase-types/src/database.ts` loses the seven generated lines for the removed function.

**Not changed — the three things 162-10 did that this is not undoing.**

- **Window 267 stays closed.** The `user_can ('entity', id, 'nomination.read')` disjunct is present on
  all four authenticated SELECT policies, byte-for-byte as 162-10 wrote it. Measured in § 5.
- **The twenty `user_can` conversions stand.** No policy reverted to `has_role` or `can_access_project`;
  05-organization-admin.test.sql TEST B asserts the absence of all five retired predicates and is green.
- **The D-21 renames stand.** `entity_update_own_candidates` and `entity_update_own_organizations` are
  untouched, and 18-entity-policies.test.sql's self-update-family assertion is green.

### Why `nomination_entities_confirmed` was reverted too, and not left calling the composition

The ruling says "the composition is REVERTED to the pre-162-10 shape" and moves V-6 from (A) to (C).
162-10's V-6(A) made `entity_is_anon_visible` the one composition called by the four anon policies, the
four authenticated ones **and** `nomination_entities_confirmed`. Leaving the third caller alive would
have kept the function in existence — a **ninth** spelling of the rule that 162-17's commissioned guard
explicitly does not cover, since that guard derives its eight from `pg_policies`.

It would also have left a depth-3 path in place. `nomination_entities_confirmed` is itself
`SECURITY DEFINER` and is called once per nomination row by `anon_select_nominations`; calling the
composition from inside it made the helpers depth 3. **Measured: 625.6 ms composed against 291.0 ms
reverted**, 5000 anon-visible nominations, same fixture. Since D-36's whole causal claim is that depth is
the regression, leaving that path composed would have contradicted the ruling it implements.

The restored body is **byte-identical** to `a45d767b4`'s (`md5 d1d58caa53b86f6051a5ddbfa093b7be` both
sides), so this is a revert and not a rewrite. It restores 162-08's recorded accepted cost — the
terms-of-use rule written in two places — together with the mitigation that was written for it: the
biconditional in `16-anon-visibility.test.sql`, which is untouched and green.

**916 of 921 stayed green.** That is the behaviour-neutrality of the revert, stated as an observation.

### Assertion 46 — replaced, and strictly stronger

162-10's form asserted that two named anon policies reach the project term *directly or through the one
composition*, and that the composition's own body still requires `project_open_for_voters`, read from
`pg_get_functiondef`. With the composition gone the second clause is unsatisfiable by construction —
there is no function whose definition could carry the term. The operator released this assertion for the
second time and the replacement is stronger in four ways:

1. **Derived, not enumerated.** It reads *every* `TO anon` SELECT policy on the four entity tables from
   `pg_policies`. A fifth entity table, or a renamed policy, is judged by it automatically. 162-10's form
   named two policies and would have missed the `anon_select_factions` perturbation in § 6(a) entirely.
2. **Both helpers, not one call.** Each qual must name `project_open_for_voters` **and**
   `entity_has_confirmed_nomination`, so a qual that keeps the project term and quietly loses the
   nomination term now reddens. The old form accepted a single delegating call.
3. **Population pinned, not just the pass count.** It asserts `'4/4 1/1 1'`, not `4`. A clean answer
   cannot come from an empty result set.
4. **The function-body clause survives, pointed at the function that now holds the rule.**
   `nomination_entities_confirmed` must still require **both** helpers in its own `pg_get_functiondef`,
   so a term dropped from that function cannot hide behind a policy that merely calls it. That was the
   sharpest clause of 162-10's form and it is kept.

It also adds `NOT LIKE '%entity_is_anon_visible%'`, so the composition cannot return unremarked. It
deliberately does **not** assert that the eight quals are identical to each other: that is 162-17's
commissioned guard, and writing half of it here, in a fenced file, by a different hand, is what the D-35
investigation stopped rather than do.

**Every other assertion in `16-anon-visibility.test.sql` is byte-identical**, and
`03-anon-read.test.sql`, `06-storage-rls.test.sql` and `07-rpc-security.test.sql` were not touched at all.

### D-21's normalised-identity assertion over the SELECT family — re-pointed, not re-derived

**The withdrawn claim.** Under V-6(A) all eight quals delegated to one call, which is what made the
SELECT family collapse to one string. Under D-36 the rule is assembled in each qual and `candidates`
alone carries the two terms-of-use conjuncts — a property of that table and of no other. The four
authenticated SELECT predicates are therefore **two** expressions modulo the table name and the type
literal, not one. **This is D-36's own recorded cost and is not repaired here.**

**What was written in its place**, at both sites (18-entity-policies test 53, 05-organization-admin
test 16): the normaliser additionally strips the exact string
`' AND (terms_of_use_accepted IS NOT NULL) AND (terms_of_use_accepted < now())'`, the message says so in
full, and — because an assertion that removes text before comparing is exactly the kind that lies — each
site gained a **new companion assertion pinning the stripped term to its population**:

- 18-entity-policies: the stripped term is carried by `'2/8'` — two of the eight entity SELECT policies,
  both of them `candidates`'.
- 05-organization-admin: the stripped term is carried by exactly `2` policies across the four tables.

A strip that matched nothing, or a terms-of-use conjunct leaking onto a second table, reddens there.

**Test 51 was re-pointed rather than deleted**, for the same reason. Its two properties — SECURITY
DEFINER with a pinned `search_path`, and D-21's "the entity type is an ARGUMENT" — did not go away; only
the functions they are asserted *of* changed. It now reads `'2/1/0'`: both surviving helpers are
SECURITY DEFINER with a pinned `search_path`; `entity_has_confirmed_nomination` takes the entity type as
its first argument; and **`entity_is_anon_visible` does not exist**, so the composition cannot be
reintroduced by a later plan without something saying so.

**Test 15 (TEST B) was strengthened, not relaxed**: `LIKE '%entity_is_anon_visible%'` became two positive
terms (`project_open_for_voters`, `entity_has_confirmed_nomination`) plus
`NOT LIKE '%entity_is_anon_visible%'` added to its list of forbidden names.

**Assertion count: 921 -> 923.** The two added assertions are the population floors above; no assertion
was deleted.

**V-6 now sits at its own option (C), not (A).** D-35's equivalence clause is withdrawn with the

## 9. The instrument lies if you do not fingerprint it

The D-35 report's § 8 records a 19x error caused by benchmarking "the shipped form" by applying nothing,
against a database an earlier experiment had left in another state. That trap was avoided here:

- `yarn db:reset` (exit 0) was run **before** the before-numbers and **after** every perturbation —
  seven resets in all.
- Every timing prints the md5 of the eight quals and of every relevant function body, taken **inside** the
  timing transaction. The before-run's `entity_is_anon_visible` fingerprint is `baceba3d…`, which is the
  same value the D-35 report recorded for the shipped body — independent confirmation that the
  before-column measured the shipped form and not a residue.
- Every fixture is built and rolled back inside one transaction, and every timing reports its row count.
- After the final reset the applied eight-quals md5 is `bf8d3dcf…`, equal to the after-probe's — so the
  committed schema is the one the numbers describe.

One further trap, recorded because it is specific to this repo: **`yarn test:db` before `yarn db:types`
contaminates `packages/supabase-types/src/database.ts`.** The order used here was `db:reset` ->
`db:types` -> `test:db`, and the generated diff is the seven lines of the removed function and nothing
else.
