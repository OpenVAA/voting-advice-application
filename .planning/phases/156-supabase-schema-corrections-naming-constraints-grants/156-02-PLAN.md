<objective>
Rename the role vocabulary in both SQL copies: the second member of `public.user_role_type` becomes
`organization`, and every SQL site that names the old term moves with it.

This is roadmap criterion 1 / REVIEW-DB-01's SQL half. The defect being corrected is an asymmetry the
codebase already resolved once: `public.entity_type` has read `'candidate', 'organization', 'faction',
'alliance'` for a long time, and only the ROLE vocabulary still says otherwise. The rename makes the
two agree.

Measured scope: **18 lines carrying the term in `apps/supabase/supabase/schema/`, 18 in
`apps/supabase/supabase/migrations/00001_initial_schema.sql` — the same 18 lines, twice.** Both copies
move in the same commit; the parity gate from plan 01 is now standing to catch it if they do not.

Purpose: the RLS predicate surface reads the role out of a JWT claim and compares it to a literal. A
policy left comparing the retired literal does not error — it silently returns false, and every
organization admin silently loses access to their own organization. That is why this plan ends with a
rebuilt database and a green pgTAP run, not with a clean grep.

Output: the renamed enum, six renamed RLS sites including the policy's own identifier, five renamed
email-helper sites, four renamed comments, a regenerated type projection, and the suite's first
enum-label assertion.
</objective>
