This is the last piece of roadmap criterion 1 / REVIEW-DB-01. After it, every layer named in D-E1
option (a) — enums, schema, migrations, dev-seed, frontend — carries the renamed vocabulary.

**Measured boundary, and it is the whole difficulty of this plan.** `packages/dev-seed/src` has 84
lines carrying the term as a word, across 22 files — but they are three different kinds of thing, and
only two of them may move:

- **Comments and prose** — move.
- **TypeScript identifiers** (`PARTY_WEIGHTS`, `PARTY_CONSTITUENCY_MATRIX`, `partyIdx`,
  `partyExtId`, `partyExtIdRaw`, `partyExtIdPrefixed`, `partyByIndex`, `findPartyIndex`,
  `findAllianceForParty`, the `PartyIndex` type) — move. These are package-internal names;
  `yarn typecheck` is the detector.
- **Seeded display labels** such as the organization names in the E2E base template — **do not
  move.** Measured this session: `tests/tests/specs/voter/voter-alliance.spec.ts` asserts on those
  exact strings by visible text at lines 87, 88, 100, 105, 140 and 141. Renaming them turns this plan
  into a red E2E suite, which under CLAUDE.md's cardinal rule blocks the phase.

Purpose: the frontend sites are not cosmetic. Two of the five are role predicates — one in the
Supabase data writer and one in the candidate login route — that decide whether an authenticated user
is treated as a candidate-or-organization principal at all. Left comparing the retired literal they do
not error; they return false, and organization users silently lose the candidate app.

Output: five frontend sites moved, the seed package's comments and identifiers moved with its data
left untouched, and a green typecheck / lint / unit run against the regenerated types.
