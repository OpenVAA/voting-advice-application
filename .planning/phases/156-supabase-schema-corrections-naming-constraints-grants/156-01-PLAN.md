<objective>
Prove the phase's whole pipeline on one thin end-to-end slice, then make its principal hazard machine-checked.

The slice is roadmap criterion 7 / REVIEW-DB-07: remove `public.candidates.name` — the column that
competes with the first/last-name-derived display name — while keeping `short_name` on both entity
tables and keeping `public.organizations.name`. It is the smallest change in the phase that traverses
**every** layer the remaining nine plans will touch: `schema/` → `migrations/00001` → the third copy in
`migrations/00002` → the live database via `yarn db:reset-with-data` → regenerated
`packages/supabase-types` → pgTAP proof. If this path works, the architecture of the rest of the phase
is proven; if it does not, that is discovered after one commit instead of after nine.

Purpose: the phase's dominant risk is not any individual criterion — every one is mechanically small —
it is that a criterion lands in one SQL copy and not the other, producing a green-looking diff and an
unchanged database (`apps/supabase/README.md:26`, verbatim: "Nothing verifies that they agree"). This
plan closes that with a real gate, demonstrated red-then-green.

Output: the column drop applied in all three SQL copies; `scripts/assert-schema-migration-parity.mjs`
wired into `yarn lint:check` with a reviewed golden signature; `156-DISPOSITIONS.md` created carrying
the Contract-C3 RPC choice that Phase 157's planning is blocked on.

**Where the authority lives, for every plan in this phase.** Per D-N3, `156-CONTEXT.md` is the
per-phase decision record and `.planning/v2.15-DISCUSSION-POINTS.md` is the authority on anything it
does not restate. Per D-0.1, the discussion document's measured fact table is the factual baseline and
the roadmap entry was corrected in place against it — **where the roadmap and a measured fact disagree,
the fact wins**, because the facts were measured at a known commit and the roadmap's numbers were not.
Two consequences worth stating once, here, rather than rediscovering them later: the phase requirement
identifiers `REVIEW-DB-01` through `REVIEW-DB-08` ARE defined in `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` (the
context document's claim that they are undefined is stale, and the requirement text is what the plans
are written against); and `apps/supabase/README.md` is the in-tree statement of the two-copy structure
this plan makes machine-checked.
