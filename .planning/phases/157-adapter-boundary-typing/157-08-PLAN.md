<objective>
Make criterion 1's "a grep for casts on adapter reads returns empty" a standing, enforced check rather
than a one-time observation (REVIEW-ADP-01; CONTEXT open question 9, answered by `157-RESEARCH.md`
section A.5).

The assertion is precise and mechanically checkable: in
`apps/frontend/src/lib/api/adapters/supabase/**/*.ts`, **zero** lines match
`as\s+Json\s+as\s+unknown\s+as`. That is the exact spelling of the smell the reviewer named, it had a
measured baseline of 15 before `157-07`, and it cannot fire on the Phase-164 nullability compensations
or on the internal type assertions, none of which use the triple form.

Purpose: without this, criterion 1 closes by inspection and the class is free to reopen on the next PR.

Output: a committed Node scan script, one line in the `lint:check` chain, a chain-membership assertion
inside the script, and two ledger rows proving the guard was blind before and catches after.
</objective>
