<objective>
Two preparatory measurements that `157-15`'s guard depends on, both of which must happen while the
guard still does not exist.

**First, the probe.** `157-RESEARCH.md` section F.5 flags exactly one claim as reasoned rather than
measured: what happens to a rule's options when TWO overlapping flat-config objects both match a file.
The research's own words are that the planner must probe it, that it is a five-minute `lintText`
experiment, and that getting it wrong silently deletes a ban. The recommended guard shape is one
extended block plus one narrower second block, and that shape is only safe if the answer is known.

**Second, the blind halves of the negative control.** The milestone standing rule is *prove the guard
fails before claiming it guards*. A blindness measurement taken after the guard is installed is not a
measurement of blindness. So the ledger is created, its rows are laid out, and the OLD halves are run
here — with `apps/frontend/eslint.config.mjs` untouched.

Output: `157-NEGATIVE-CONTROL-LEDGER.md`, carrying the probe verdict, the full header field set, all
rows pre-created, and the OLD halves measured.
</objective>
