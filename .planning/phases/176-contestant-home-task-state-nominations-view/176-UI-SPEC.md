# Phase 176 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for the surfaces Phase 176 adds or reshapes in the Contestant app: the
> home screen (I) as an ordered task list with one main action, the nominations view (J), the reusable
> "not editable" note, the entries that stay closed until the entity has a nomination, the five buttons
> that follow the shared next step, and the copy that changes with them. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher,
> verified by gsd-ui-checker.
>
> **This phase adds no design language.** SvelteKit 2 + Svelte 5 runes, Tailwind 4 + DaisyUI 5, Inter,
> theme colours in `packages/app-shared/src/settings/staticSettings.ts`. The token sections below
> **document the established contract** the surfaces obey; they introduce **no new token, font, spacing
> step, colour, icon or component library**. The load-bearing content is in *Surface Contracts*,
> *Copywriting Contract*, *Locator Contract*, *Axe Scan Entries* and *UI Considerations*.

Exceptions — tokens that are not a step of the 4 / 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 scale, each an existing
token used here only as existing components already use it:

| Token | Value | Declared as | Why it is kept |
|-------|-------|-------------|----------------|
| `10` (= `md`) | 10px — not a multiple of 4 | `--spacing-10: 0.625rem` | **One use in new markup**: `pe-10` on the menu's reason line, so that its text ends where every menu item's does — `NavItem`'s own horizontal padding is `px-10` `[tree]`. | `44`, `touch` | 44px | `--spacing-44`, `--spacing-touch: 2.75rem` | The 44px target, already the minimum of every `Button` and `NavItem`. |

other gap written for a card or a sentence is a multiple of 4 (4, 8, 12, 20).

## Surface Contracts

### Shared premises

- **One state, read everywhere** `[CONTEXT D-02, D-05]`. Home, the menu, the five buttons of S6, the
  logout button and "the profile is complete" all read `contestantCtx.tasks`, `contestantCtx.nextStep` and
  the one rule for "the preview is open". No page keeps a second completeness rule. `tasks`, `nextStep`,
  the preview fact, `nominationsLocked` `[175-plan]`, `questionsReady` `[175-plan]`, `answersLocked`,
  `selectedEntity`, `profileComplete` and `appSettings` are reactive accessors: read `contestantCtx.X`
  inside the tracking scope, never destructure; `dataRoot` is never bound to a read alias (`CLAUDE.md`
  § Context Destructuring Rule; `.claude/skills/components/context-reactivity.md`).
- **Three statuses, and "not known yet" is not one of them.** A task is `complete`, `incomplete` or
  `blocked` `[CONTEXT D-02]`. Before the facts a status rests on are loaded the page renders the row as
  **pending** — a rendering of "not known yet", not a fourth status in the state. How the state says "not
  known yet" is the planner's `[CONTEXT D-05, "states its rule for 'not loaded yet' once"]`.
- **Nothing is decided from an empty list** `[RESEARCH P-4, T-6]`. "No question unanswered" is also what
  "not loaded" and "no nomination" look like: today `profileComplete` is
  `unansweredRequiredInfoQuestions.length === 0 && unansweredOpinionQuestions.length === 0` `[tree]`, true
  for an entity with nothing loaded. After this phase **`profileComplete` is "every task present is
  `complete`"** `[U-21]`, so its four readers — home's "ready" title, emoji and tip, and the
  `SuccessMessage` on the profile and on the questions list — can never greet an entity with no
  nomination, and never render from a pending state.
- **Blocked comes before omitted** `[CONTEXT D-03, R16]`. An entity with no nomination has its profile and
  questions `blocked` and shown; the questions row and menu item are left out only for an entity that has
  a nomination and is **known** to have no opinion question (175's known-zero rule `[175-plan]`). The
  question counts are not read at all while there is no nomination: a blocked entry never carries a count.
- **One page per route, one descriptor per type** `[175-plan: lib/contestant/utils/entityDescriptor.ts,
  "entityDescriptorOf"]`. No `{#if type === 'organization'}` and no ternary on the type appears in a page
  or in the menu. This phase adds four members and re-points one:

  | Member | Candidate | Organization | Read by |
  |--------|-----------|--------------|---------|
  | `homeNoNomination` | `contestantApp.home.ingress.noNomination.candidate` | `….organization` | S2 |
  | `closedReasonNoNomination` | `contestantApp.common.closedReason.noNomination.candidate` | `….organization` | S2, S3 |
  | `nominationsEmpty` | `contestantApp.nominations.empty.candidate` | `….organization` | S4 |
  | `nominationParentLabel` | `common.electionList` | `common.alliance.singular` | S4 |
  | `nominationsDescription` (re-pointed) | `contestantApp.nominations.description.candidate` | `….organization` | S4 |

  `electionSymbolLabel`, `greetingName`, `homeNotDone`, `homeNotDoneNoQuestions` and
  `hideIfMissingAnswersKey` are 175's members `[175-plan]`, read here unchanged.
- **Vocabulary** `[175-UI-SPEC U-25]`. The UI never says "entity" or "organization": an organization is a
  **party**, the thing a user edits is a **profile**. A user with no nomination is not at fault: home and J
  say what is missing and whom to ask, and never use the vocabulary of an error — no hero emoji from
  `dynamic.error.heroEmoji`, no `ErrorMessage`, no warning colour on the empty state.
- **RTL is out of scope on this branch.** New markup uses logical utilities where it sets a side (`ps-44`,
  `pe-10`, `text-start`), so the RTL branch's port stays mechanical.

### S1 — What a task row and a closed entry look like

**The three tasks** `[CONTEXT D-03]`, in this order, for both entity types.

| Task | Test id of its `Button` | Its own icon | Label: incomplete or blocked / complete / answers locked | Count |
|------|------------------------|--------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|-------|
| nomination | `contestant-home-nomination` (new) | `election` | `contestantApp.home.nomination.enter` / `.view` / — the answers lock does not change it `[U-11]` | none |
| profile | `contestant-home-profile` | `profile` | `contestantApp.home.basicInfo.enter` / `.edit` / `.view` (existing keys) | unanswered required info questions |
| questions | `contestant-home-questions` | `opinion` | `contestantApp.home.questions.enter` / `.edit` / `.view` (existing keys) | unanswered opinion questions |

**Status → rendering** `[U-04]`. The status is told three ways at once — the leading icon's shape, the
badge, and a sentence for assistive technology that is part of the button's own name — and never by
colour alone.

| | `complete` | `incomplete` | `blocked` | pending (not known yet) |
|---|-----------|--------------|-----------|-------------------------|
| Leading icon | `check` | the task's own icon | `locked` | the task's own icon |
| Badge (`Button`'s `badge` snippet) | none | `InfoBadge` in its warning form: the count when it is above zero, otherwise the literal `!` | none | none |
| The `Button` | a link to the task's page | a link to the task's page | `disabled`: no `href`, `aria-disabled="true"`, not a tab stop | a link to the task's page |
| Label | the "complete" label | the "incomplete" label (or the "answers locked" one) | the "incomplete" label | the "incomplete" label |
| Status sentence (`sr-only`, inside the `Button`) | `contestantApp.home.taskStatus.complete` | `…taskStatus.incompleteCount` with the count, or `…taskStatus.incomplete` when there is none | `…taskStatus.blocked` | none |
| `aria-describedby` | none | none | the reason sentence's id | none |
| `data-status` on the `<li>` | `complete` | `incomplete` | `blocked` | `pending` |

- **The status sentence is read once.** It is an `sr-only` `<span>` rendered in the row's `badge`
  snippet, which `Button` renders inside its own element whenever the button has an icon `[tree:
  Button.svelte, "{@render badge?.()}"]`. It is therefore part of the button's accessible name — "Not
  completed yet: 3 items left. Enter your basic information" — heard once, in browse mode and on focus
  alike. There is no sibling status text beside the button and no `aria-describedby` for the status. A
  complete or a blocked row renders the snippet with the sentence alone.
- The badge is wrapped in `aria-hidden="true"`: its figure, and the `!`, are a visual mark, and the status
  sentence says the same in words. Today the bare number is read as part of the button's name `[tree]`.
- The `!` is a symbol, not a catalogue string. It marks an incomplete task that has nothing to count: the
  nomination, and a profile that lacks only its required name `[175-plan: the name clause of
  profileComplete]`.
- A count badge is shown whether or not answers are locked, as today.

**A closed entry says why** `[CONTEXT D-15]` `[U-05]`. Exactly one **reason sentence** is on screen
whenever an entry is closed, in Info type at full contrast, directly after the entries it explains in
reading order, and every closed entry's `aria-describedby` names its id. The description is announced
because each closed entry has a role: on home `Button` gives every row `role="button"` `[tree]`; in the
menu `ContestantNav` passes one (S3).

| The entity | Closed entries | Reason sentence | `data-reason` |
|------------|----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| has no nomination | profile, questions, preview | `contestantApp.common.closedReason.noNomination.<type>` | `noNomination` |
| has a nomination; a required info question or an opinion question is unanswered | preview | `contestantApp.common.closedReason.preview`, or `…previewNoQuestions` for an entity known to have no opinion questions | `previewNotReady` |
| has a nomination and both are filled | none | not rendered | — |

"The preview is open" is one rule `[CONTEXT D-03]` `[U-03]`: a nomination, no unanswered required info
question, no unanswered opinion question. The required name does not close it `[OD08-D7]`, so an
unconfirmed party with no default-language name can have an incomplete profile row and an open preview.

### S7 — A closed URL

`[CONTEXT D-13, D-14]` The profile, the questions and a question page redirect on the server (307) to the
entity's home while the entity has no nomination; the preview does so too while a required info question
or an opinion question is unanswered. The questions are **not** closed on missing required info.

  are known; the page once there is an opinion question — **whether or not required info is filled**; and
  175's neutral no-questions page on a known zero `[175-plan]`. The "`<Loading />` for the instant before
  the redirect" of `175-UI-SPEC` S5 no longer exists.

  list and its reason sentence say what is missing. Nobody is signed out; the `candidateNoNomination`
  message leaves the login page with its code `[CONTEXT D-16]`.
- **Where focus lands.** Home renders `MainContent`'s default `<h1 tabindex="-1">` in every state, pending
  included, so the navigation target exists on the first frame.

  | Arrival | Focus | Announcement |
  |---------|-------|--------------|
  | A client-side navigation that the server redirects (a stale entry, a link from another page) | Home's `<h1>`, by the root layout's post-navigation focus reset (`focusNavigationTarget`: `[data-focus-on-nav]`, else the first `<h1>` `[tree]`) | `#route-announcer` speaks home's title |

  Focus is never left on `<body>` and never placed on a page that was not rendered `[RESEARCH RQ-1 (e), A5]`.

  removes a nomination or adds a required question meanwhile, the user clicks an entry home shows open and

**U-01 — "Save and Continue" on a question page while basic info is missing** (OQ-1). **Departs from the letter of roadmap criterion 4.**
Criterion 4: "Every Continue button leads to the entity's next incomplete task — basic info before the opinion questions". `[CONTEXT D-06]`: "`nextStep` is the first incomplete task." The operator's EDIT lets a user answer questions before the basic info is filled, a state no source describes. `[CONTEXT D-06]` also says the question page's resume-at-first-unanswered rule "must survive".
- [ ] ★ (a) A Continue inside the questions continues the questions: while an opinion question is unanswered it leads to the first unanswered one; when none is left it leads to the first incomplete task — the profile if it is incomplete — and home when nothing is. Saving an edit of an already-answered question, with none unanswered, returns to the questions list, as today. Home's main action still names the profile first.
- [ ] (b) **Criterion 4 to the letter:** after every saved answer the user is taken to the profile while basic info is missing, and back to the questions by hand.

**U-02 — Where the questions task leads from home and from the profile's save** (OQ-2). **Departs from `[CONTEXT D-06]` as written.**
`D-06`: "When that task is the questions, its route is the first unanswered question … It reproduces what each Continue site does today". On today's tree two of the four sites go to the questions **list** (`href: resolveRoute('ContestantAppQuestions')` in the home helper; `submitRoute: getRoute.current('ContestantAppQuestions')` on the profile), and the journey's steps 14 and 15 assert the list and its intro.
- [ ] ★ (a) The questions task carries two targets: its page (the list), taken by home's main action, the profile's save and J's Continue; and the first unanswered question, taken by the two buttons inside the questions. Nothing a user sees today changes. Accepting it amends `D-06` to "…its route is the questions list from outside the questions, and the first unanswered question from inside them".
- [ ] (b) **`D-06` as written:** every site goes to the first unanswered question. Home and the profile's save skip the questions' intro page, and journey steps 14 and 15 are rewritten.

**U-03 — What "both filled" means for the preview** (OQ-3; marked as inferred in the context).
- [ ] ★ (a) A nomination, no unanswered required info question, and no unanswered opinion question. The required name does not count `[OD08-D7]`, so an unconfirmed party without a default-language name has an incomplete profile row and an open preview. An entity that is asked no opinion question has its preview open on the required info alone.
- [ ] (b) "Basic info filled" also means the required name — the preview then stays closed for a party that cannot yet be named, against the reading of `OD08-D7`.

**U-04 — How a row on home shows its status.**
- [ ] ★ (a) By its leading icon — `check` when complete, the task's own icon when incomplete, `locked` when blocked — plus the warning badge on an incomplete row (the count, or `!` when there is nothing to count) and a status sentence for screen readers, inside the button's own name so that it is heard once. The rows are an `<ol>` without visible numbers. The bare count is no longer read as part of the button's name.
- [ ] (b) A visible status word at the end of each row ("Done", "To do", "Not available yet") — three more strings in seven locales, and a second column that long labels must share at 320 px.
- [ ] (c) As today: the verb of the label and the count badge alone; "complete" and "blocked" get no mark of their own.

**U-05 — How a closed entry says why, and to whom.**
One sentence at full contrast under the closed entries, on home and in the menu, in every option. The options differ in how the menu's closed items reach assistive technology: a disabled `NavItem` link is an `<a>` with no `href`, no `role` and no `tabindex`, on which `aria-disabled` and a description are announced to nobody.
- [ ] ★ (a) **`ContestantNav` passes the role.** On its three items, while closed: `role="link"`, `aria-describedby` to the reason line, and the dimming class. `NavItem` is not changed. Reach: the Contestant menu alone; the voter menu's disabled items and the current language in the language list stay as they are — undimmed and not announced as disabled.
