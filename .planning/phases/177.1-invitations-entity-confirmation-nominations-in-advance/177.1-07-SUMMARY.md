New subsection **The confirmation**: `p_confirm_entity` is required with no default; a call without it finds no function and `null` is refused. `true` confirms each named entity of the batch as the caller (with the existing definition of "has a name"); `false` confirms no one. No call unconfirms an entity, no item overrides the call's value, a project-scope item confirms nothing, and deleting the invitation leaves the entity confirmed. `SupabaseAdminClient.createInvitations` of `@openvaa/dev-seed` passes `true` unless told otherwise (`{ confirmEntity: false }` confirms no one). The visibility sentence and the name-freeze sentence (with its link) are kept, and the visibility sentence now ends "so an invitation changes what voters see only through what its call states".

New subsection **The nominations**:
- The key is for an entity-scope candidate or organization item, at most 1000 objects. It is refused on a project-scope item and for a faction or an alliance, and one entity's nominations may be stated on one item only.
- A key table: `set_entity_nominations`' keys (linked to the authentication page's section) plus `confirmed` (required), `id` (optional; updates that nomination), `election_round` (optional; the current round when absent), `custom_data` (without `requestedParentOrganization`) and the writable columns.
- Every id is a uuid. An `id` object moves nothing, and unstated columns keep their stored values.
- The nominations are "created or updated, never deleted", in the call's transaction and as the caller. They pass the same private functions as `set_entity_nominations`, and the policies, column grants and triggers decide each write. A caller without the authority is refused, and nothing of the batch is written. The confirmation is read back, raising `42501` on a mismatch.
- A parent of another project is refused. A parent's nomination the call creates is unconfirmed, and an administrator confirms it as a separate act. "A confirmed nomination under an unconfirmed parent nomination is therefore a state an administrator can produce."
