## Assessment of the items the orchestrator asked about

**WR-01 (existence oracle).** Confirmed by reading the code: the `private.entity_project_id` check (SECURITY DEFINER) runs at line 230, before the invitations INSERT at 394 whose policy `admin_insert_invitations` decides authority. An authenticated caller with no authority can send `nominations: []` for a (project, uuid) pair and tell 22023 ("no entity of the project") from the 42501 of the policy, so learns whether a uuid is a candidate or organization of that project. Before the phase, such a call failed with 42501 either way. Judgment: it does not undermine the phase goal. The goal and criterion 2 require that an unauthorised caller is refused with nothing written, and that holds on both branches; confirmation behaviour and what voters see are not touched. It is a narrow information disclosure: the caller must already hold the unguessable uuid and a project id, and learns one bit about it, no content. It is not in the security register's 35 threats (T-177.1-16 covers the cross-project write, which the check does close), so SECURITY.md `threats_open: 0` remains true as written, but the register does not cover this disclosure. It sits below the `high` blocking threshold. Recommend fixing it in a follow-up (a read as the caller, or moving the check after the INSERT, as the review proposes) rather than blocking; the disposition is still `open`, which is the one thing that should be recorded as a decision.

**Plan 04 t1 verify (two single-line greps).** Prettier and the type generator wrap the lines the greps expected on one; the facts the greps stood for are verified above (signature in the migration, `p_confirm_entity` in the types). Not a goal gap.

**Plan 07 t3 link audit (30 dangling citations).** Pre-existing, unrelated to the phase's content; the `docs-links` gate row is 0. Not a goal gap.

**Journey step 2.5 about 2 s slower (undiagnosed).** The step passes 3/3 in isolation and in the full run; no behavioural claim depends on its duration. Not a goal gap; an undiagnosed cause stays unconfirmed, as the rules require.

**Voter-app rendering of a confirmed nomination under an unconfirmed parent nomination (unobserved, todo filed).** Not a goal gap for this phase.
