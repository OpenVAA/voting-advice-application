<assumption_delta_decision>
**The noun that is now primary:** the **(matcher x operator-position x punctuator) cell** — not the
matcher.

**Decision: `add-alongside`.**

**Rationale:** the deterministic scan's `fallback` / `parameterised` signals are prose artifacts of the
verification history and carry nothing. The real delta is the one the checkpoint's own note names: this
guard is organised as one regular expression per NAME, while the defect class that has now recurred
eight times is a two-axis matrix — a punctuator spelling at an operator position. Eight reviews have
each found one cell; none could find the empty ones, because nothing enumerates the axes. So the cell
becomes a first-class, enumerated noun ALONGSIDE the named matchers, which stay exactly as they are:
they are what the guard executes, what the vacuity probes mutate, and what a reader greps for.

**What would force a promote:** if the declarations and the enumeration ever disagree in a way the
completeness assertion cannot express — for instance if a matcher needs a position the canonical-shape
template cannot represent — then the cell matrix becomes primary and the regex literals become its
output rather than its input. That is deliberately NOT done now: generating six regular expressions
from a table would cost the vacuity probes their verbatim pin and the reader their grep.

**The contract test the delta invites** is plan 161-16 in its entirety: a derived cell enumeration that
goes red the instant a future phase reintroduces the singular assumption — by widening a position no
fixture exercises, or by leaving a position of the canonical shape with no disposition at all.
</assumption_delta_decision>
