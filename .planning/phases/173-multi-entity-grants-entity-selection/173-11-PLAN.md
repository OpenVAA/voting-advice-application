<objective>
Stage 5 (multi-grant E2E, axe, docs, closing gate), part 1 of 3: the seed side, fixtures first. Widen the
suite's base dataset to both entity types, re-baseline what the app's new name changes, and add the three
dedicated rows the selection spec will edit (D-20; decision X1 (a); ruling O-Q1).

without moving a single voter assertion. Both are dataset facts; landing them alone shows that the widening,
not the new spec, is what any moved assertion answers to.

gate run and one full E2E run.

## Rulings and deviations carried by this plan

1. **Ruling O-Q1.** D-20 speaks of choosing two base candidates no other spec's user edits. Three rows are
   added for the purpose instead. They are base rows nobody else edits, they are unconfirmed and so
   invisible to voters, and a write to them cannot move a voter assertion. Choosing two visible candidates
   would need a proof, per voter spec, that nothing the selection spec writes is ever read.
2. **`MINIMAL_BASE_APP_SETTINGS` is left candidate-only** (D-20's discretion). The perm family tests
   voter-side settings and candidate sessions; it keeps one dataset family on the single-type name.
3. **A visual baseline is regenerated only if its sole difference is the app's name.** Any other difference
   stops the plan. A regenerated baseline is listed for the operator.
