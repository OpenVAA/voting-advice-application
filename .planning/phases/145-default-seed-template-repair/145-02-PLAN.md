<objective>
Build the phase's **tracer**: one thin, production-quality path that runs end to end from the CI
credential export, through a genuinely-anon Supabase client, across the RLS boundary, into the
`get_nominations` RPC, and out to an assertion — and observe it **RED against the pre-fix template**,
with its own role control **green** in the same run.

Purpose: this is the only check in the repository that crosses the boundary the voter app crosses.
Landing it before the fix is what makes "the guard catches the defect" a measured pair rather than a
claim, and it is the first half of that pair.

Output: a standing guard, a fast pure-I/O early-warning tier beside it, the CI credential wiring both
need, and ledger rows `P1-RED`, `U1-RED`, `U2` and `CI1`.
</objective>

## ⚠ THIS PLAN DELIBERATELY LEAVES THE SUITE RED

`yarn workspace @openvaa/dev-seed test:unit` is **expected to exit non-zero** at the end of this plan.
That is the point: the standing acceptance rule requires the guard to be **observed failing** against
the unfixed template before it can be claimed to guard, and `145-01` established the ordering as a
property of the commit graph. `145-04` lands the one-column fix and turns it green.

**The red must have the right shape.** In the `P1-RED` run:

- the **candidate** assertion in the new `it` fails, naming `anon-visible candidate nominations`;
- the **`accounts` role control** in the same `it` **passes** — if it fails instead, the anon
  credential is wrong and the run proves nothing about the defect. **Stop-the-line signal.**
- the **organization** assertion passes — organizations are visible to anon today, which is what
  makes the differential diagnostic rather than a blanket failure.

A red whose failure is the role control, or whose failure is on organizations, is a **void row**.
Diagnose and re-run; do not record it.

Row `U2` (`Test 29`) is a **must-NOT-fire** row: it is expected **GREEN here and GREEN after the
fix**, because it constrains `e2e/base`, which this phase does not change.
