## Rulings and deviations carried by this plan

1. **The tracer comes after the dataset and the fixtures, and after the three plans of parts** (execution rules, "Three orders"). It is the first plan whose result a browser can reach.
2. **The tracer task holds more than five files, and they land in one commit.** `routeConsistency.test.ts` requires every route key under the protected group to name a directory on disk and every page directory there to have a route key; a step's page links to the next step's key. The three keys and the three directories therefore exist together or not at all. The files are small and each has its analog; the pure rules they call were built and tested in plans 177-13 and 177-14.
3. **The loads are universal** (item P-09): `+layout.ts` and `+page.ts` that take the root load's data through `await parent()` and build a temporary data root, as the voter's selection loads do. The nominations view's facts are therefore in the server-rendered markup, and no extra database read is made.
4. **The guard's tracer condition** — an entity with a nomination in the editor's reach is sent to the nominations view — is a stated, located stub: one condition of the guard, one case of its test, both removed by plan 177-17.
5. **The page reaches the writer through two functions on the context**, `setEntityNominations` and `getNominationParentOptions`, each bound to the selected entity, as the context's other writer calls are. They are stable functions, not reactive accessors.
6. **RESEARCH assumption N-2 is built on as settled**: the context's data root is empty in the server render, so each step shows `Loading` first. Assumptions A3 (one `tick()` suffices) and A4 (the page stays mounted through the rerun) are pinned by plan 177-18's component test; here the fallback to the entity's home covers a state that is not yet known.
7. **What this plan does not build**, each named with its plan: the start-from-constituency mode's sentence (177-16); the prefill from existing rows, the "kept" section, existing, moved and removed cards and the duplicate-parent rule (177-17); the label's full table, the named refusals, the lock found by a refused save, and the leave-with-unsaved-changes prompt (177-18); the entry from the home and from the nominations view (177-19).
