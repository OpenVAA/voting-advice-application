## Task 1 — the over-disclosure audit and the one restructure

`157.1-OVER-DISCLOSURE-AUDIT.md` enumerates **53** `error` call sites in the non-test tree. **40 interpolate** into their message (39 single-line plus one multi-line at `results/[[electionTab]]/+layout.svelte:158-160`, which a single-line grep undercounts). Each is dispositioned:

- **1 restructured** — `isValidResult.ts`, the only site that is simultaneously server-reachable, on the settings path this phase rebuilt, and interpolating a **provider** error rather than an application-authored one.
- **12 deferred** with a named remediation each, plus 3 non-constant-message adjacents. The strongest is `api/cache/+server.ts:70`, which interpolates `url.searchParams.get('resource')` — unbounded, attacker-chosen, validated only as a parseable URL — straight into production stdout. That is a log-injection surface as much as a disclosure one, and it is the highest-priority item in the document.
- **26 kept**, each read and closed: client-only (`$effect` / DOM handler, so the record reaches only the console of the session that produced it) or carrying a code-authored constant or a UUID.

**The level census.** `error` 53 · `warn` **0** · `info` 0 · `debug` 36. The document states that the empty `warn` tier is decision **C5(b)**'s intended end state, that no record may be invented to fill it, that no gate may count levels, and that `'warn'` must remain the production **threshold** even though nothing emits at it — threshold and emitted level are different things, and the headroom is what lets a future `warn` record appear without a deployment-wide config change. Pitfall **P7** exists because an empty tier reads like a regression to anyone who did not read the decision.

**The restructure.** Message became the constant `INVALID_RESULT_MESSAGE`; the branch became a flat `reason` attribute; the provider error is neither interpolated **nor** passed as `err` — `serialiseError` would carry both its `message` and its `stack` into the same sink, which moves the disclosure rather than removing it. The boolean contract is identical for every input, asserted case by case.
