## Issues Encountered

**The measurement harness could not import the spec's fixtures directly**, because the spec is a TypeScript module that executes `describe()` at load. Re-typing the twelve fixtures into the harness would have made the OLD and NEW halves measurements of a COPY of the spec rather than of the spec — precisely the borrowed-observation failure the register forbids. Resolved by having the harness read the spec off disk and slice its `PRELUDE`, `SELECTORS`, `MESSAGES`, `VIOLATIONS` and `CLEAN` declarations out of the source text. First attempt desynchronised on an apostrophe inside a JSDoc comment (`fact 5's exact line`), which the bracket scanner read as a string delimiter; fixed by stripping comments before scanning. Both halves of row 6 were then taken through the identical extraction.

## Observations for later phases — recorded, NOT acted on

- **The guard cannot see `outcome.value ?? {}`.** `supabaseDataProvider.ts:99` and `:132` both coalesce a parse outcome to an empty object. Both are DELIBERATE and both are documented in-file as collapses taken at the point of consumption, after the record was already emitted, into a return type that has no third state to carry the distinction into — they are not defects. But the fifth selector cannot distinguish them from a degrade, because its left operand must be a `parse*` CALL and theirs is a member expression. That is the concrete reason decision D2 was right to reject the coalesce ban as the whole ban, and it is also the honest limit of what a green lint proves here.
- **A candidate for the two compiler-blind sites (WINDOWS.md 209/210), offered and not pursued.** `supabaseDataProvider.ts:365`'s `Record<string, unknown>` sink and `supabaseDataWriter.ts:355`'s `as unknown as` double assertion are both syntactically distinctive and both inside this block's `files` glob, so a `TSAsExpression[typeAnnotation.typeName.name='unknown'] > TSAsExpression` selector and a `TSTypeReference[typeName.name='Record']` selector scoped to return positions would be cheap sixth and seventh entries. **Not added here** — that is a different defect class from the one requirement D8 names, it would need its own fixture set and its own measured false-positive scoring, and widening this plan to chase it is exactly the scope creep the plan warned against.
- **The spec's `SELECTORS` constant is a second copy, and nothing asserts it equals the config's.** The transcription check only proves each `undefined` arm kept its type guard. A case comparing `calculateConfigForFile`'s resolved selectors against `SELECTORS` would lock the transcription itself. It is not in the plan's behaviour list and was not added; the fixtures firing through the REAL config is what actually proves the config, and this would only catch a drift that made the spec's copy stale. Offered for `157.1-08` or a later hardening pass.
- **One pre-existing lint warning is untouched.** `candidateContext.svelte.test.ts:19` reports an unused `question` binding at WARNING tier. It is outside the adapter, unrelated to this plan's changes, and does not affect any exit code. Not fixed, per the scope boundary.
