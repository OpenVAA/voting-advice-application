<decisions>
## Decisions

- **D-01 (operator decision D1 (a)):** the fix is its own small phase, after 174 and before 175.
- **D-02 (operator decision D2 (a)):** scope is **both defects, plus one E2E check of the gate at phone
  width**. The check is part of the default suite; without it the overflow can come back unseen.
- **D-03 (from the todo's Solution, not ruled by the operator):** the overflow is fixed in the component
  that forces the width, not by clipping or hiding overflow on an ancestor; the focus defect is fixed by
  moving focus to the navigation target after the gate's `invalidateAll()`, with a component case.
- **D-04 (roadmap criterion 3):** the phone-width check is shown to fail on the overflow as it stands
  before the fix — a control, run once on the unfixed tree or on a tree with the fix reverted — so that a
  green check means something.
- **D-05 (operator, 2026-10-07, after the research):** the overflow fix is **wrap everywhere** — the consent
  label in `TermsOfUseForm.svelte` gets `whitespace-normal`, overriding DaisyUI's `.label { white-space:
  nowrap }`, which the research measured as the one rule that forces the width (`174.1-RESEARCH.md` RQ-1).
  The expander is not touched. The operator chose this over wrapping below 640px only (which leaves the
  checkbox off-screen at 640–740px in four locales) and over widening the column. Consequence accepted: in
  English at 1280px the sentence wraps to two lines and the content above moves up about 8px; roadmap
  criterion 4 is amended to say so. This supersedes the "1280px stays as it is" line under *Out of scope*
  for that one wrap.
- **D-06 (research recommendation, not ruled by the operator):** the focus fix goes in the shared gate
  mechanism — the protected layout requests `focusNavigationTarget()` when the view it rendered changes,
  except on first render — so that Phase 178's gates are covered without code in any gate. The
  handler-level variant also measured correct and is the fallback.
- **D-07 (research recommendation, not ruled by the operator):** the phone-width check is a new spec in a
  new leaf project, two tests (320 and 390), asserting no horizontal overflow against
  `documentElement.clientWidth`, the checkbox fully in the viewport, and that it can be checked — never
  clicking Continue, because the stored session is shared with the axe scans.
</decisions>

<factual_baseline>
## What was measured, and by whom

From `.planning/todos/pending/2026-10-06-173-terms-of-use-view-phone-overflow-and-focus.md` (a browser
probe during Phase 173, plan 10). **Taken from that record; not re-measured when this file was written.**

- At 390 x 844 on the gate view at `/contestant`: `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` 483 against
  `clientWidth` 390. The four widest elements under `<main>` are 576px wide: the form's `section`, its
  `div.mb-lg`, the `collapse` expander and the expander's `input.min-h-0`.
  `[data-testid="terms-checkbox"]` has its left edge at x = −93, stable over six reads. Content left of the
  viewport cannot be scrolled to, so the box cannot be ticked.
- At 1280px there is no overflow.
- After Continue, `document.activeElement` is `BODY` on the page that replaces the gate: the gate continues
  with `invalidateAll()`, which is not a navigation, so the root layout's `afterNavigate` focus move does
  not run. A user with one entity at the root is redirected after the gate, which is a navigation, and is
  not affected.

## Not established — research items

- **RQ-1:** which element forces the 576px width (the expander and its input are the candidates), and why
  the checkbox ends at a negative x rather than the content overflowing to the right.
- **RQ-2:** whether the defect predates Phase 172's move of the form into a gate view.
- **RQ-3:** whether the registration page's use of the same form has the same overflow. The route tree
  under `contestant/register/` is being rebuilt by Phase 178; say what exists today.
- **RQ-4:** whether other users of the expander component overflow at phone width. If they do, that is
  reported, and fixed here only where the same one-component fix covers it.
- **RQ-5:** which of the three outcomes after the gate (the selection screen, the "nothing to edit" view,
  an entity page reached through an entity URL) lose focus, and what `focusNavigationTarget()` focuses on
  each.
- **RQ-6:** where a phone-viewport check fits: no E2E project runs the Contestant App at a phone width, and
  the axe scan of the view (`contestant-terms-gate` in `tests/tests/specs/a11y/contestant-a11y.spec.ts`)
  runs at 1280 x 720. Options to weigh: a viewport override inside one spec, a phone-viewport entry in the
  scan, or a new project. The voter app's specs may already show the house way to set a viewport.
</factual_baseline>
