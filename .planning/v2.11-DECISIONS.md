- **Temporary bridges/shims are allowed only during Waves 1–3** (they're what keeps consumers compiling until the codemod runs) and **must all be deleted by Phase 98 (Wave 4).** End state: zero `svelte/store` bridges.
- **No migration-era prefixes survive in shipped code** — no `rune…` / `…Native` / `…2` suffixes. Rune-native replacements take over the **original file + symbol names** in place.
